DEVELOPER AND API TERMS

Version 1.0 · Effective Date: 10 September 2026 · Last Updated: 10 September 2026

These Developer & API Terms ("Developer Terms") are Supplemental Terms governing access to and use of BACI developer services, application programming interfaces, software development kits, sample code, developer documentation, sandbox environments, credentials, webhooks, integrations and related developer functionality ("Developer Services").

These Developer Terms apply whether Customer uses Developer Services for internal applications, Customer-facing applications, integrations, automation, AI or agentic systems, or products and services made available to permitted third parties.

Developer Services can provide programmatic access to valuable data, intelligence and operational capabilities. Accordingly, access is conditioned on secure credential management, documented use, lawful data handling, respect for technical limits and the authority boundaries established by the Agreement.

1. INCORPORATION AND PRECEDENCE

These Developer Terms form part of the Agreement and BACI Legal Framework.

For matters expressly concerning Developer Services, these Developer Terms govern as applicable Supplemental Terms, subject to the Agreement's order of precedence.

The DPA remains authoritative for Processing Customer Personal Data on Customer's behalf. The Security Policy / Security Addendum governs contracted security obligations. The AI & Autonomous Systems Terms govern AI and Autonomous functionality. An applicable Order may expressly establish additional or different Developer entitlements.

2. SCOPE

These Developer Terms apply to BACI APIs, SDKs, command-line tools, libraries, code samples, developer portals, documentation, sandbox and test environments, API credentials, OAuth clients, webhooks, event interfaces, integration frameworks and other functionality BACI designates for developers.

They do not grant rights in unrelated BACI Services or permit use outside the applicable subscription, documentation or Order.

3. DEFINITIONS

“API” means a BACI application programming interface made available for programmatic access.

“API Credential” means a key, secret, token, certificate, OAuth credential or other authentication material used to access Developer Services.

“Application” means software, integration, service, workflow or other technology developed, operated or controlled by Customer that accesses Developer Services.

“Developer Data” means data Customer or its Application submits to or receives from Developer Services, excluding BACI Technology.

“End Customer” means a third party for whom an authorised Developer Application provides permitted functionality.

“Production” means live use beyond development, testing or evaluation.

“Sandbox” means a non-production environment intended for development, testing or evaluation.

Other capitalised terms have the meanings in the Terms, DPA or applicable Supplemental Terms.

4. ELIGIBILITY AND AUTHORITY

Customer represents that the person accepting these Developer Terms has authority to bind the applicable Customer.

Customer must maintain a valid BACI Account, subscription, plan or other entitlement required for the Developer Services used.

BACI may require additional verification before enabling Production credentials or higher-risk capabilities.

Subject to the Agreement, BACI grants Customer a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable licence during the applicable Service term to access and use Developer Services solely to develop, test, operate, support and maintain Applications for Customer's permitted business purposes.

No ownership in BACI Technology transfers to Customer.

Rights not expressly granted are reserved by BACI.

6. DOCUMENTATION

Customer must use Developer Services in accordance with applicable BACI technical documentation, supported authentication methods, endpoint requirements and published technical restrictions.

Documentation may evolve as Developer Services change.

Documentation does not amend a higher-precedence contractual commitment unless the Agreement expressly incorporates the relevant technical specification as binding.

7. APPLICATION REGISTRATION

BACI may require Customer to register an Application, identify its purpose, authorised domains, redirect locations, requested scopes, environments or other technical information.

Registration information must be accurate and kept current.

BACI may refuse, restrict or revoke a registration that is materially misleading, insecure, prohibited or inconsistent with Customer's entitlement.

8. API CREDENTIALS

API Credentials are confidential security credentials.

Customer must not publish, disclose, sell, transfer, share or embed credentials in public code, public repositories, client-side code or other locations where unauthorised persons can reasonably obtain them.

Customer is responsible for protecting credentials and for activity performed using credentials within Customer's control, subject to the Agreement's allocation of responsibility for security incidents and BACI-controlled failures.

9. CREDENTIAL ENVIRONMENTS

BACI may issue separate Sandbox and Production credentials.

Sandbox credentials must not be treated as Production credentials unless BACI expressly supports that use.

Customer must not use a credential in an environment or manner inconsistent with its designated purpose.

10. CREDENTIAL ROTATION AND REVOCATION

BACI may rotate, expire, revoke or require replacement of API Credentials for security, legal, operational or contractual reasons.

Customer must promptly rotate or revoke credentials reasonably suspected of compromise.

Applications should be designed to accommodate credential rotation without unsafe workarounds.

11. OAUTH AND DELEGATED ACCESS

Where BACI supports OAuth or comparable delegated authorisation, Customer must request only scopes reasonably necessary for the Application's functionality.

Customer must accurately represent requested permissions to users and must not use a token beyond the authority granted.

Revoked, expired or reduced authorisation must be respected promptly.

12. LEAST PRIVILEGE

Customer should configure Applications according to least-privilege principles.

An Application's technical ability to request or invoke a permission does not establish Customer's legal authority to use that permission.

Customer is responsible for ensuring that access to data and actions is proportionate to the Application's legitimate purpose.

13. CUSTOMER RESPONSIBILITY FOR APPLICATIONS

Customer is responsible for Applications it develops or controls, including their design, code, security, user experience, legal compliance, data practices, instructions, Outputs, actions and interactions with BACI.

BACI's provision of APIs, SDKs, sample code or documentation does not constitute approval or certification of Customer's Application.

14. END-CUSTOMER APPLICATIONS

Where Customer's plan permits Applications serving End Customers, Customer is responsible for its relationship with those End Customers.

Customer must provide legally required terms, privacy disclosures, permissions and support.

Customer must not represent that BACI is the direct provider of Customer's Application or has endorsed it unless BACI expressly authorises that representation.
