AGENCY AND ADVISER AGREEMENT

Version 1.0 · Effective Date: 10 September 2026 · Last Updated: 10 September 2026

This Agency & Adviser Agreement ("Agreement") governs participation by an agency, advisory firm, consultancy, professional adviser or other approved service provider ("Partner") that uses BACI to support work for its own clients.

This Agreement is designed for BACI's Agency and Advisory access categories. It permits Partner to operate authorised client workspaces within BACI while preserving clear responsibility between BACI, Partner and each client. It does not make Partner BACI's agent, reseller, employee, fiduciary, franchisee or legal representative and does not authorise Partner to bind BACI.

Each client workspace is a governed environment. Partner must have authority from the applicable client to provide data, configure BACI, obtain intelligence, operate workflows and take or recommend actions for that client.

1. INCORPORATION AND PRECEDENCE

This Agreement forms part of the BACI Legal Framework and incorporates the Terms of Service and applicable BACI policies and Supplemental Terms.

The DPA governs Personal Data processed by BACI on behalf of the applicable Customer. The Security Addendum governs contracted security obligations. The AI & Autonomous Systems Terms govern AI and Autonomous functionality. The Billing, Refund and Cancellation Policies govern their respective subjects.

An executed Order, SOW, Enterprise Customer Agreement / MSA or amendment controls where the BACI order of precedence gives it priority.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Partner must be a bona fide business or professional practice authorised to provide the services it offers to clients.

BACI may require business, identity, compliance, sanctions, ethics or authority verification before activating or continuing Agency or Advisory access.

Partner must promptly notify BACI of a material change that could affect eligibility, authority, compliance or risk.

3. AUTHORITY TO ACCEPT

The person accepting this Agreement represents that they have authority to bind Partner.

Partner is responsible for ensuring that its Authorised Users comply with this Agreement.

Acceptance is on behalf of the Partner entity, not merely the individual user.

4. AGENCY AND ADVISORY ACCESS

Agency and Advisory access is intended to permit Partner to use BACI in connection with professional services supplied to multiple clients, subject to Partner's plan, Mode, Capacity and Order.

Independent Professional access is not intended for a multi-client workspace practice where BACI requires Advisory access.

Partner may not use Agency or Advisory access outside the commercial rights expressly granted.

5. CLIENT WORKSPACES

Partner must maintain each client in the appropriate separate BACI workspace or organisation structure provided for the applicable plan.

Partner must not intentionally commingle one client's Confidential Information, Personal Data, strategy, Outputs or private materials with another client's workspace except where all affected clients have lawfully authorised the arrangement and BACI supports it.

Workspace separation is a governance control and must not be deliberately circumvented.

6. FIRST CLIENT WORKSPACE

Where BACI onboarding requires a first client workspace, Partner must configure a genuine client workspace with accurate business context before completing onboarding.

Partner must not create a fictitious client merely to bypass onboarding.

The first workspace is subject to the same authority, confidentiality and data requirements as later client workspaces.

7. CLIENT AUTHORITY

Before placing Client Data in BACI or using BACI to act for a client, Partner must have sufficient contractual or other lawful authority from that client.

Partner is responsible for the scope of its mandate, including authority to access client systems, process data, configure markets and competitors, generate intelligence, connect integrations and initiate actions.

BACI does not independently verify the underlying professional mandate unless BACI expressly agrees to do so.

8. NO AUTHORITY TO BIND BACI

Partner has no authority to enter contracts, make warranties, incur liabilities, grant rights, issue refunds, alter BACI pricing or make commitments on BACI's behalf.

Partner must not state or imply otherwise.

Any BACI commitment must come from BACI through an authorised BACI representative or applicable BACI agreement.

9. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS

BACI and Partner are independent contractors.

Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, employment relationship, franchise, fiduciary relationship or general agency between BACI and Partner.

Neither party may bind the other except through express written authority.

10. PARTNER–CLIENT RELATIONSHIP

Partner is independently responsible for its professional relationship with each client, including its engagement letter or services agreement, scope, Fees, taxes, professional duties, deliverables, advice, communications and client support.

BACI is not a party to the Partner–client engagement merely because BACI is used to perform it.

11. CLIENT TERMS

Partner must ensure its client relationship contains terms sufficient to authorise Partner's lawful use of BACI and Client Data.

Partner must not use its client agreement to purport to impose obligations on BACI beyond the BACI Agreement.

Where a client contracts directly with BACI, the client's BACI agreement governs its direct rights against BACI.

12. DISCLOSURE OF BACI USE

Partner must not misrepresent BACI-generated intelligence, AI functionality or automation as wholly human-created where disclosure is required by law, professional standards or the nature of the engagement.

Partner should disclose material reliance on BACI where necessary for informed client decision-making.

Partner may accurately describe BACI as a technology provider but may not imply BACI has independently endorsed Partner's advice.

13. PROFESSIONAL JUDGMENT

Partner remains responsible for professional judgment, client advice and recommendations it provides.

BACI Intelligence may support but does not replace professional duties applicable to Partner.

Partner must apply review proportionate to consequence, uncertainty and the standards governing its profession.

14. REGULATED PROFESSIONS

If Partner provides legal, financial, investment, accounting, medical, engineering, regulated procurement or other regulated professional services, Partner is responsible for licences, registrations, supervision, independence rules and professional standards applicable to its practice.

BACI does not confer a professional licence or authorise Partner to practise a regulated profession.

15. CONFLICTS OF INTEREST

Partner is responsible for identifying and managing conflicts of interest among its clients.

BACI workspace separation does not constitute a professional conflict check.

Partner must not use one client's confidential intelligence to benefit another client where doing so would violate confidentiality, fiduciary, contractual or professional obligations.

16. CLIENT CONFIDENTIALITY

Partner must protect Client Data and client Confidential Information using safeguards appropriate to its sensitivity.

Partner must restrict access to personnel who require it for the client engagement.

BACI's confidentiality obligations are governed by the BACI Agreement; Partner's independent confidentiality obligations to its client remain Partner's responsibility.

17. CLIENT DATA

As between BACI and the applicable Customer, rights in Client Data are governed by the Terms and DPA.

Partner represents that it and the applicable client have all rights and permissions necessary for Client Data submitted through Partner's BACI access.

Partner must not submit data obtained unlawfully or outside its client mandate.

18. PERSONAL DATA ROLES

The legal roles of BACI, Partner and client depend on the Processing context.

Where BACI Processes Personal Data on behalf of a Customer, the DPA applies. Partner is responsible for determining and documenting its own controller, processor, service-provider or other role in relation to its clients.

This Agreement does not deem every party to have the same privacy role in every workflow.

19. CLIENT PRIVACY INSTRUCTIONS

Partner must comply with lawful client instructions concerning Personal Data within the scope of Partner's engagement.

Partner must not instruct BACI to Process Personal Data in a manner Partner knows is unlawful.

Where a request concerns Customer-controlled Personal Data, BACI may direct the request through the appropriate Customer or DPA process.

20. DATA SUBJECT REQUESTS

Partner is responsible for handling data-subject requests it receives in its own legal capacity.

Where BACI is a processor and assistance is required, the DPA governs BACI's obligations.

Partner must not promise a BACI response time or capability beyond the DPA or applicable Agreement.

21. SECURITY

Partner must maintain reasonable administrative, technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the client information it handles.

Partner is responsible for its devices, users, credentials, identity systems, integrations and environments.

BACI's security obligations are governed by the Security Addendum and Agreement.

22. ACCESS CONTROL

Partner must provision BACI access according to least privilege and client need.

Personnel should not retain access to a client workspace after their role ends.

Partner must promptly revoke access for departed personnel or others who no longer require it.

23. CREDENTIALS

Partner must keep BACI credentials confidential and must not share individual credentials among personnel.

API Credentials are governed additionally by the Developer & API Terms.

Partner must promptly report suspected credential compromise to security@bacihq.com.

24. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Partner must promptly notify BACI of a security incident within Partner's control that materially affects BACI Services, BACI credentials or data held in BACI.

Partner must reasonably cooperate with containment and investigation.

BACI's notification obligations for a BACI Security Incident are governed by the DPA and Security Addendum.

25. CAPACITY

Partner's plan may limit clients, workspaces, markets, competitors, users or other resources.

Partner must remain within contracted Capacity and must not create duplicate or artificial structures to evade limits.

BACI may refuse additional configuration beyond Capacity until Partner upgrades or purchases additional entitlement.

26. ADVISORY CAPACITY

Where applicable, Advisory plans may provide Capacity appropriate to a professional adviser operating client workspaces.

The actual Capacity shown in Partner's Order, plan or BACI entitlement controls.

Capacity is not transferable to another BACI customer unless BACI expressly permits it.

27. AGENCY CAPACITY

Where applicable, Agency plans may provide broader client, market or competitor Capacity than Advisory plans.

The applicable Order or entitlement controls the actual allowance.

Agency status does not create reseller, licensing or Technology Partner rights.

28. CLIENT ONBOARDING

Partner is responsible for collecting accurate client business context needed for BACI configuration, including authorised market, competitor, objective and other information.

Partner must not knowingly enter materially false client information.

BACI may rely on information supplied by Partner when configuring intelligence.

29. CLIENT OFFBOARDING

When a client engagement ends, Partner must remove or restrict Partner personnel access and follow applicable instructions concerning data return, transfer or deletion.

BACI-hosted data remains governed by the Agreement, DPA and Data Retention & Deletion Policy.

Partner must not retain client data merely because Partner's BACI subscription continues if Partner lacks independent authority to retain it.

30. TRANSFER OF CLIENT WORKSPACE

A client workspace may be transferred, separated or converted to direct client control only where BACI supports the process and the relevant parties have authority.

BACI may require verification, a new Order, client acceptance of BACI terms or technical migration.

Partner may not sell or assign BACI's contractual relationship with a client without BACI's consent.

31. BACI INTELLIGENCE

Partner may use BACI Intelligence within the scope of its permitted professional services.

Partner is responsible for contextualising intelligence for the client and for any advice or action Partner derives from it.

BACI Intelligence remains subject to the accuracy, probabilistic and third-party-data provisions of the Terms.

32. CUSTOMER-SPECIFIC OUTPUT

Rights in Customer-specific Output are governed by the Terms.

Partner may provide permitted Output to the client for whom it was generated as part of Partner's services.

Partner must not treat underlying BACI Technology, methodologies, models, taxonomies, scoring systems or intelligence engines as transferred to Partner or client.

33. CROSS-CLIENT BENCHMARKING

Partner may not use one client's private data or confidential Output to benchmark or advise another client unless Partner has lawful authority from the affected client and the use complies with applicable confidentiality and privacy obligations.

BACI may provide aggregated or deidentified functionality as permitted by the Agreement.

34. AI FUNCTIONALITY

Use of BACI AI Functionality is governed by the AI & Autonomous Systems Terms.

Partner must apply professional and human review proportionate to the significance of the advice or action.

Partner must not present probabilistic AI Output as guaranteed fact where uncertainty is material.

35. NO GENERAL MODEL TRAINING BY DEFAULT

Unless the applicable Customer expressly agrees in writing or affirmatively opts into a clearly identified programme, BACI will not use Customer Confidential Information, Customer Personal Data, private Inputs or private Outputs to train or fine-tune general-purpose or shared AI models for the benefit of other customers.

Agency or Advisory access does not weaken this protection.

The DPA remains authoritative for Customer Personal Data.

36. AUTONOMOUS FUNCTIONALITY

Where Partner uses Autonomous functionality for a client, Partner must have authority from the client for the relevant class of actions and must configure appropriate Authority Boundaries.

Partner must not infer authority merely because an integration technically permits an action.

Autonomy does not remove governance.

37. CLIENT APPROVALS

Partner is responsible for determining which decisions require client approval under the client engagement, professional standards or law.

Hybrid and Autonomous functionality must be configured consistently with those requirements.

BACI is not responsible for Partner's failure to obtain a client approval that Partner was required to obtain.

38. FINANCIAL AUTHORITY

Partner must not configure BACI to commit client funds, make purchases, place bids, submit applications or take other financially consequential actions unless Partner has sufficient client authority.

Partner should use spending limits, approval thresholds and other controls where supported.

Technical access does not equal unlimited financial authority.

39. PROCUREMENT, GRANTS AND FUNDING

Where Partner uses BACI for procurement, bids, grants, funding, investors or similar opportunities, Partner remains responsible for client eligibility, submission accuracy, representations, certifications, deadlines and legal compliance.

BACI discovery or analysis does not guarantee award, funding, investment or eligibility.

40. MARKETING AND CAMPAIGNS

Where Partner uses BACI for marketing, campaign, audience, creator, affiliate or distribution activities, Partner is responsible for applicable advertising, privacy, consumer-protection, platform and communications laws.

Partner must not use BACI to conduct deceptive, unlawful or prohibited campaigns.

41. CLIENT COMPETITOR INFORMATION

Partner must obtain and use competitor information lawfully.

BACI does not authorise trade-secret theft, unlawful surveillance, credential misuse, deceptive access or other prohibited intelligence collection.

The Acceptable Use Policy applies.

42. REPRESENTATIONS ABOUT BACI

Partner may accurately describe BACI's publicly documented capabilities.

Partner must not make false or unsubstantiated claims about BACI accuracy, regulatory status, security certifications, guaranteed outcomes, exclusivity, pricing or functionality.

Partner must not promise roadmap features on BACI's behalf.

43. BACI BRAND

Partner may use BACI names and marks only as expressly authorised by BACI brand guidelines or written permission.

Partner must not register confusingly similar names, domains or marks.

Any approved use must preserve BACI's ownership and reputation.

44. PARTNER BRAND

BACI does not acquire ownership of Partner's name, marks or branding merely because Partner participates in Agency or Advisory access.

Any public use by BACI requires the permission provided in an applicable Order, programme term or separate authorisation.

Neither party may imply an endorsement beyond the actual relationship.

45. WHITE-LABELLING

Agency or Advisory access does not automatically include white-label rights.

Partner may not remove required BACI attribution, represent BACI Technology as Partner-owned technology or rebrand BACI as a standalone Partner platform unless BACI expressly grants those rights.

Platform & Intelligence Licensing Terms may apply to authorised licensing arrangements.

46. RESALE

Partner may charge clients for Partner's professional services, including services that use BACI, but may not resell BACI subscriptions or raw platform access unless BACI expressly authorises resale.

Partner's service Fee is independent of BACI's direct pricing.

Technology Partner or reseller rights require the applicable separate agreement.

47. REFERRALS

A client introduction does not create a referral commission unless Partner participates in an applicable BACI referral or affiliate programme and satisfies its terms.

Partner may not claim commission under this Agreement alone.

Affiliate compensation is governed by the Affiliate Programme Agreement.

48. FEES TO CLIENTS

Partner determines its own professional Fees and billing arrangements with clients.

BACI is not responsible for collecting Partner's client Fees unless a separate written arrangement expressly provides otherwise.

Partner must not state that BACI sets or approves Partner's professional Fees.

49. BACI FEES

Partner must pay BACI Fees associated with Partner's BACI subscription, Capacity, usage and other purchases according to the Billing Policy and Order.

Partner remains responsible for BACI Fees regardless of whether a client pays Partner, unless BACI has contracted directly with that client for the relevant charge.

50. TAXES

Each party is responsible for taxes allocated to it under the applicable Agreement and law.

Partner is independently responsible for taxes arising from Partner's client services and invoices.

BACI does not provide Partner tax advice.

51. CLIENT PAYMENT DISPUTES

A dispute between Partner and a client concerning Partner's Fees does not suspend Partner's payment obligations to BACI.

Partner must resolve its own client collections.

BACI will not adjudicate Partner–client Fee disputes merely because BACI was used in the engagement.

52. PROFESSIONAL INSURANCE

Partner is responsible for maintaining insurance required by law, professional standards, client contracts or reasonable practice for Partner's business.

BACI may require evidence of specified insurance for particular enterprise, regulated or high-risk engagements where an Order or programme requirement states it.

BACI does not insure Partner's professional advice.

53. DUE DILIGENCE

BACI may conduct reasonable due diligence concerning Partner's identity, ownership, sanctions exposure, government relationships, compliance history, professional standing or proposed BACI use.

Partner must provide materially accurate information and update material changes reasonably relevant to BACI's compliance obligations.

BACI may decline or end participation where risk cannot reasonably be managed.

54. ANTI-BRIBERY AND CORRUPTION

Partner must comply with applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws.

Partner must not offer, promise, authorise or provide improper payments or anything of value in connection with BACI business, client procurement or public officials.

Partner must maintain reasonable controls appropriate to its risk.

55. GOVERNMENT OFFICIALS

Partner must identify and manage legal obligations when acting for or interacting with government officials, state-owned entities or public-sector customers.

Partner must not use BACI to facilitate improper influence, undisclosed conflicts or unlawful payments.

The Government & Public Sector Schedule applies where incorporated.

56. SANCTIONS AND EXPORT CONTROLS

Partner must comply with applicable sanctions, export controls and trade restrictions.

Partner must not use client structures, routing, intermediaries or false information to evade restrictions.

BACI may restrict access where required by law.

57. COMPETITION LAW

Partner must comply with applicable competition and antitrust laws.

Partner must not use BACI or BACI-facilitated relationships to coordinate unlawful pricing, market allocation, bid rigging or improper exchange of competitively sensitive information.

58. MODERN SLAVERY AND HUMAN RIGHTS

Partner must not use BACI in connection with human trafficking, forced labour, exploitation or other prohibited conduct.

Partner should maintain controls proportionate to its operations and legal obligations concerning human rights and supply-chain integrity.

59. RECORDS

Partner must maintain records reasonably necessary to demonstrate its authority and compliance for client work performed through BACI.

BACI may maintain Partner, workspace, usage, billing, security and compliance records according to the Data Retention & Deletion Policy and Applicable Law.

60. COMPLIANCE INFORMATION

BACI may request reasonable information needed to verify compliance with this Agreement, client-workspace entitlements, Capacity, security or applicable law.

BACI will not use this right to demand unrelated client trade secrets without legitimate need.

Any formal audit right in a negotiated agreement controls where applicable.

61. TRAINING

Partner is responsible for ensuring its BACI users are appropriately trained for the functions they perform.

BACI may provide onboarding, documentation or training resources.

Completion of BACI training does not constitute professional certification unless BACI expressly identifies a programme as such.

62. PARTNER PERSONNEL

Partner is responsible for personnel and subcontractors it permits to access BACI.

Partner must ensure such persons are bound by confidentiality, security and data-protection obligations appropriate to their access.

Partner remains responsible for their acts and omissions to the extent provided by law and the Agreement.

63. PARTNER SUBCONTRACTORS

Partner may use subcontractors in its client services only where permitted by Partner's client obligations and Applicable Law.

Partner is responsible for assessing and contracting with its own subcontractors.

A Partner subcontractor does not become a BACI Subprocessor merely because it accesses BACI under Partner's authority.

64. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

BACI retains all rights in BACI Technology.

Partner retains rights in its independently developed methodologies, templates, materials and professional work product, subject to BACI's rights in BACI Technology and applicable Output provisions.

Neither party receives ownership of the other's pre-existing intellectual property merely through this Agreement.

65. PARTNER MATERIALS

Partner represents that it has rights necessary to provide materials it submits to BACI.

BACI may Process Partner materials only as permitted by the Agreement.

Partner remains responsible for third-party restrictions applicable to materials supplied by Partner.

66. FEEDBACK

Partner may voluntarily provide feedback concerning BACI.

Unless otherwise agreed, BACI may use that feedback without payment or restriction, provided doing so does not transfer ownership of Partner Confidential Information, Client Data or Partner's independently developed proprietary materials.

67. CONFIDENTIAL BACI INFORMATION

Partner must protect non-public BACI information according to the confidentiality provisions of the Terms or applicable negotiated agreement.

Partner must not disclose BACI credentials, non-public security information, proprietary methods, pre-release functionality or confidential commercial information except as authorised.

68. SUPPORT

BACI support is provided according to Partner's plan, applicable support policy, SLA or Order.

Partner remains the primary support contact for its own professional services and client-specific advice unless BACI has a direct support relationship with the client.

Partner must not promise BACI response times beyond contracted commitments.

69. SERVICE LEVELS

Binding service levels exist only where an applicable SLA or Order expressly provides them.

Partner may not pass through a stronger BACI SLA to a client than Partner actually receives unless Partner assumes that obligation independently.

SLA credits are governed by the SLA.

70. SERVICE CHANGES

BACI may evolve Services subject to the Terms and applicable contractual commitments.

Partner is responsible for adapting its professional processes to supported functionality.

Partner must not promise clients indefinite availability of a particular uncontracted feature.

71. SUSPENSION

BACI may suspend Partner or affected workspaces on grounds permitted by the Terms, including security risk, prohibited use, non-payment, legal requirement or material breach.

Where reasonably practicable, BACI will seek to limit suspension to the affected user, workspace or Service.

Suspension does not eliminate accrued Fees.

72. CLIENT-SPECIFIC SUSPENSION

BACI may restrict a particular client workspace without necessarily suspending Partner's entire account where the issue is client-specific.

Partner must cooperate with reasonable remediation.

BACI may require proof of authority or compliance before restoring access.

73. TERM

This Agreement begins when accepted or otherwise incorporated into Partner's BACI relationship and continues while Partner has applicable Agency or Advisory access, unless terminated earlier under the Agreement.

Individual Orders may have separate terms.

74. CANCELLATION AND NON-RENEWAL

Partner may stop future renewal according to the Cancellation Policy and applicable Order.

Cancellation of Partner's BACI subscription does not itself terminate Partner's independent obligations to its clients.

Partner is responsible for planning client transition before BACI access ends.

75. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE

Termination rights for material breach are governed by the Terms and applicable negotiated agreement.

Where a cure period applies, the terminating party must follow it unless immediate termination is permitted.

Refund consequences are governed by the Refund Policy and applicable Agreement.

76. EFFECT OF TERMINATION

Upon termination of applicable access, Partner must stop using BACI except for surviving rights and must cease representing itself as an active BACI Agency or Advisory customer where that representation would be misleading.

BACI may revoke credentials and access.

Accrued Fees and provisions intended to survive remain enforceable.

77. CLIENT TRANSITION

Partner should maintain reasonable transition plans for active clients if Partner's BACI access ends.

BACI is not obligated to continue Partner access solely because Partner has ongoing client contracts.

Where a client wishes to contract directly with BACI, BACI may establish a separate relationship subject to client acceptance and applicable commercial terms.

78. DATA RETURN AND DELETION

Data return, retention and deletion are governed by the DPA, Terms and Data Retention & Deletion Policy.

Partner must distinguish between its own data rights and rights belonging to a client.

Partner must not instruct BACI to return Client Data to a person Partner knows lacks authority.

79. WARRANTIES

Warranties concerning BACI Services are those stated in the Terms and applicable Agreement.

Partner independently warrants to BACI that it has authority for its client use, will comply with law and will not misrepresent its relationship with BACI.

BACI does not warrant Partner's professional services.

80. CLIENT OUTCOMES

BACI does not guarantee that Partner or a client will obtain revenue, contracts, grants, investment, procurement awards, customers, conversions, regulatory approvals or other business outcomes.

Partner must not make such guarantees on BACI's behalf.

81. INDEMNITIES

Indemnity obligations are governed by the Terms and applicable negotiated agreement.

Without creating duplicative recovery, Partner remains responsible for third-party claims arising from Partner's professional services, lack of client authority, unlawful Partner instructions, Partner materials or Partner's breach to the extent allocated by the Agreement and Applicable Law.

82. LIABILITY

Liability between BACI and Partner is governed by the Terms, including applicable exclusions, caps, enhanced caps and non-excludable liabilities.

Partner's separate liability to its clients is not assumed by BACI merely because Partner used BACI.

Partner must not contractually promise that BACI will indemnify or compensate Partner's client unless BACI expressly agrees.

83. NO THIRD-PARTY BENEFICIARY BY DEFAULT

A Partner client does not become a third-party beneficiary of this Agreement solely because Partner uses BACI for that client, except where an applicable Order or mandatory law expressly provides otherwise.

A direct BACI customer retains rights under its own BACI agreement.

84. ASSIGNMENT

Assignment is governed by the Terms and applicable Order.

Partner may not transfer its BACI subscription or Agency/Advisory status to another firm merely by transferring a client book or engagement.

BACI may require the successor entity to establish its own BACI account and agreement.

85. CHANGES TO THIS AGREEMENT

BACI may update this Agreement prospectively in accordance with the Terms.

A lower-precedence update will not override an expressly negotiated higher-precedence provision where the BACI order of precedence prevents that result.

Mandatory notice requirements will be followed.

86. INTERPRETATION

Agency and Advisory access authorises a professional-services use model; it does not appoint Partner as BACI's legal agent.

“Client” identifies the recipient of Partner's services and does not automatically make that client BACI's Customer.

Technical access, workspace administration or Autonomous capability does not establish professional, financial or legal authority beyond Partner's actual mandate.

87. CONTACT

Agency and Advisory account matters may be directed through BACI's applicable account or support channel.

Access enquiries: access@bacihq.com

Billing: billing@bacihq.com

Privacy: privacy@bacihq.com

Security: security@bacihq.com

ANNEX I — RESPONSIBILITY MATRIX

BACI is responsible for BACI-controlled Services, contracted security obligations, BACI-selected Subprocessor governance, BACI's contractual data-use commitments, and BACI support according to the applicable plan or Order.

Partner is responsible for its professional services; client contracts; client authority; professional judgement; client approvals; workspace administration; Partner personnel; Partner-selected subcontractors; lawful Client Data; Partner devices and credentials; and Partner's own regulatory obligations.

Client is responsible, as applicable, for instructions and approvals within the Partner–client relationship; rights in Client Data; business decisions; connected systems under client control; and obligations allocated to the client by its agreement with Partner or BACI.

The applicable Agreement controls where responsibility is expressly allocated differently.

ANNEX II — CLIENT WORKSPACE GOVERNANCE

For each client workspace, Partner should maintain:

1. correct client identity; 2. documented engagement authority; 3. authorised Partner users; 4. appropriate markets and competitors; 5. client objectives; 6. data classification appropriate to the engagement; 7. least-privilege access; 8. applicable client approval requirements; 9. AI and Autonomous Authority Boundaries; 10. spending or execution limits where relevant; 11. connected-system permissions; 12. client-specific retention requirements; 13. conflict-of-interest controls; 14. offboarding ownership; and 15. a current Partner contact responsible for the workspace.

Workspace governance should be reviewed when the client scope materially changes.

ANNEX III — AGENCY / ADVISORY RIGHTS MATRIX

PERMITTED SUBJECT TO PLAN — operating separate client workspaces; using BACI Intelligence in professional services; generating client-specific Outputs; configuring client markets and competitors; using authorised AI functionality; using Hybrid or Autonomous functionality where included and authorised; and charging clients for Partner's professional services.

REQUIRES SEPARATE BACI AUTHORISATION — resale of BACI subscriptions; white-labelling BACI as Partner technology; sublicensing BACI Technology; acting as BACI's sales agent; binding BACI; Technology Partner integration rights; platform licensing; or using BACI marks beyond applicable brand permissions.

PROHIBITED — fictitious client workspaces; commingling private client data without authority; using one client's Confidential Information for another without lawful authority; credential sharing; Capacity evasion; unlawful intelligence gathering; false BACI claims; or other Acceptable Use Policy violations.

ANNEX IV — CLIENT ONBOARDING CHECKLIST

Before activating a client workspace, Partner should confirm:

Partner has a valid client engagement; • the client identity is accurate; • Partner has authority to provide relevant data to BACI; • privacy roles have been assessed; • required client notices or consents are in place; • Partner users are authorised; • market, competitor and objective information is accurate; • sensitive or regulated data is supported by the applicable BACI environment; • integrations are authorised; • approval thresholds are defined; • Autonomous Authority Boundaries are documented where applicable; • professional conflicts have been checked; • the client understands material BACI use where disclosure is required; and • offboarding responsibility is identified.

ANNEX V — COMPLIANCE BASELINE

Partner should maintain a compliance programme proportionate to its business and BACI use, including as applicable:

anti-bribery and corruption controls; • sanctions and export screening; • conflicts management; • privacy and data-protection governance; • information security; • professional licensing and supervision; • competition-law compliance; • government-interaction controls; • records management; • subcontractor oversight; • incident response; and • training for personnel using BACI.

BACI may impose additional controls for particular jurisdictions, regulated deployments or public-sector engagements through an applicable Order or schedule.
