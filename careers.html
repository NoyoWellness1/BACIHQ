CAREERS

BACI is expanding its global commercial network.

As our reach grows across markets, industries and organisations, we are looking for people who understand business, recognise opportunity and know how to build relationships that lead somewhere.

Some opportunities are commercial. Others are technical, strategic or relationship-led. Each has its own responsibilities, requirements and structure.

OPPORTUNITIES

Every role here is commission based. Open a role to see what counts as qualifying business for it and how commission is calculated.

COMMERCIAL CONSULTANTS

Develop new business across BACI Business and Independent Professional markets, from identifying the right organisations to guiding the commercial conversation from first engagement through to conversion, and earn on the you generate. Ideal for natural relationship-builders who thrive on the chase.

CHANNEL CONSULTANTS

Build relationships with advisory firms, agencies and consultancies that can use BACI to strengthen the work they deliver for their own clients, identifying where BACI can strengthen outcomes across their client portfolios, and earn on the you generate. Suited to people who already move in professional advisory networks.

ENTERPRISE CONSULTANTS

Develop strategic opportunities with larger organisations where decisions involve multiple stakeholders and commercial priorities, leading complex conversations from initial engagement through to agreement, and earn on the you generate. Best suited to consultants comfortable navigating long, multi-stakeholder sales cycles.

LICENSING CONSULTANTS

Identify and qualify organisations with the potential to operate, deploy or commercialise BACI through Platform Licensing, Territorial Rights, Vertical Rights, Engine Licensing or Intelligence Feeds, and earn on the you generate. BACI retains responsibility for licensing scope, commercial terms and final approval. A good fit for those who understand structured, rights-based deals.

TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP CONSULTANTS

Develop relationships with technology companies, platforms and integrators, identifying where BACI intelligence, APIs and capabilities can strengthen another product or ecosystem, and earn on the you generate. Well suited to people fluent in both technology and partnership deal-making.

DEVELOPER ADVOCATES

Help developers understand what they can build with BACI through technical content, documentation, education and community engagement, and earn on the you generate. A technical community role rather than a conventional sales position, ideal for builders who enjoy teaching other builders.

ACCOUNT AND RENEWAL CONSULTANTS

Build the relationship after the initial agreement is signed, supporting customer continuity, retention, renewal and appropriate expansion, and earn on the you generate. Focused on long-term relationships rather than new customer acquisition, for people who'd rather grow an account than chase the next one.

REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT CONSULTANTS

Develop BACI's commercial presence within a particular country, market or professional network, combining local market understanding with relationship development, and earn on the you generate. The role does not confer territorial or licensing rights. A strong fit for those with deep roots in a specific local market.

DIGITAL MARKETING CONSULTANT

Develop BACI's commercial presence across digital channels and convert audience engagement into commercial opportunity. Build visibility, create content, engage relevant business audiences and earn on the you generate. Great for creators and marketers who want their content to translate directly into income.

HOW COMMISSION WORKS

Individual business basis

Commission is calculated separately for each qualifying business generated by the Consultant. Each qualifying business has its own commission lifecycle beginning on the date that business first becomes a paying BACI customer.

There is no portfolio-wide commission pool. A Consultant who generates multiple qualifying businesses may earn commission separately on each one.

Year 1, acquisition commission

For originating roles, Year 1 consists of the first 12 months of each qualifying business's paying relationship with BACI. The applicable role-specific Year 1 commission is calculated on qualifying net revenue actually received by BACI.

Year 2, continuity commission

Where applicable, Year 2 consists of months 13–24 of that individual qualifying business's paying relationship with BACI. Continuity Commission is payable only while the customer remains active, paid and in good standing.

If the customer downgrades, commission is calculated on the qualifying net revenue actually received at the lower level. If the customer cancels during Year 2, Continuity Commission ends when qualifying payments to BACI end. Annual accelerator percentages do not carry into the Continuity Commission.

End of originating commission

Unless a role-specific agreement expressly provides otherwise, originating commission ends after the completion of the qualifying business's second 12-month commission period. No perpetual commission, residual interest or ownership interest in a BACI customer is created.

Annual accelerators

Annual accelerator performance is measured over each rolling twelve months from the Consultant's start date, resetting on their anniversary. Nobody is measured on a shorter period because of when in the year they joined.

Resetting the annual accelerator does not affect commission already being earned on qualifying businesses generated in previous years. Accelerated rates apply prospectively after the applicable threshold has been reached and are not retrospectively applied to earlier qualifying business.

Qualifying net revenue

Unless a role-specific structure states otherwise, commission is calculated on qualifying net revenue actually received and cleared by BACI. Qualifying net revenue excludes, as applicable: taxes, VAT and similar government charges; refunds; credits; chargebacks; reversed payments; unpaid or written-off invoices; discounts not actually received by BACI; pass-through costs; and other amounts BACI does not retain as revenue.

Attribution

A Consultant must originate or otherwise qualify for attribution of the business under the requirements of their specific role. BACI may require opportunities to be registered through an approved attribution system.

Where more than one person claims the same opportunity, BACI will determine attribution using the documented origin and development of the opportunity. No commission is created merely by identifying an organisation already known to, registered with or actively engaged by BACI.

Payment

Commission becomes payable only after BACI has actually received and cleared the corresponding qualifying customer payment. Commission calculations and payment schedules will be administered in accordance with the applicable BACI Consultant Terms of Engagement.

Any refund, reversal or chargeback relating to previously commissioned revenue may be deducted from subsequent commission payments where permitted by the applicable terms and law.

Expansion

Where an existing customer increases its BACI spend, expansion commission is calculated only on qualifying incremental revenue where an Account and Renewal Consultant or another expressly authorised Consultant materially generated that expansion. The originating Consultant's Year 2 Continuity Commission remains calculated on the qualifying continuing revenue to which that entitlement applies.
