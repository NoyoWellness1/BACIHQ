This Intellectual Property & Content Rights Policy ("Policy") explains ownership, licensing, permitted use and protection of intellectual property, content, data, Inputs, Outputs, BACI Intelligence, brand assets and related materials within the BACI ecosystem.

This Policy is designed to preserve both sides of that relationship: Customer ownership of Customer Data and appropriate rights in Customer-specific Outputs, and BACI ownership of the underlying BACI Technology and reusable intellectual property that makes the Services possible.
