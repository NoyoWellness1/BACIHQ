TECHNOLOGY PARTNER AGREEMENT

Version 1.0 · Effective Date: 10 September 2026 · Last Updated: 10 September 2026

This Technology Partner Agreement ("Agreement") governs participation by an approved technology company, software provider, platform operator, integration provider, data provider or other technology business ("Technology Partner") that integrates, embeds, connects, interoperates with or otherwise uses BACI technology in connection with its own products or services.

Technology Partner access is a distinct BACI commercial relationship. It is not created by ordinary Developer, Agency, Advisory or Business access. The applicable Order defines the approved integration, environments, Capacity, commercial rights and any permitted End-Customer use.

This Agreement does not appoint Technology Partner as BACI's legal agent, reseller, distributor, employee, fiduciary, franchisee or representative unless a separate executed agreement expressly grants the relevant status.

1. INCORPORATION AND PRECEDENCE

This Agreement forms part of the BACI Legal Framework and incorporates the Terms of Service and applicable BACI policies.

The Developer & API Terms govern technical API and developer use unless this Agreement or an Order expressly provides a Technology-Partner-specific rule. The DPA governs Customer Personal Data processed by BACI on behalf of a Customer. The Security Addendum governs contracted security obligations. The AI & Autonomous Systems Terms govern applicable AI and Autonomous functionality.

The Agreement's order of precedence applies, including any executed Order, SOW, Enterprise Customer Agreement / MSA or expressly agreed amendment.

2. ELIGIBILITY

Technology Partner must be a bona fide organisation with the technical, operational, security and commercial capability appropriate to the proposed integration.

BACI may require due diligence concerning ownership, products, security, privacy, sanctions, compliance, financial standing, technical architecture and proposed use before approval.

Technology Partner status remains subject to continuing compliance.

3. AUTHORITY

The person accepting this Agreement represents that they have authority to bind Technology Partner.

Technology Partner is responsible for its Authorised Users, personnel and permitted contractors.

No individual may accept this Agreement on behalf of a company without appropriate authority.

4. APPROVED INTEGRATION

Technology Partner may integrate with BACI only for the use case, products, environments and commercial scope authorised by the applicable Order or BACI approval.

A technically possible integration is not automatically an approved integration.

Material expansion beyond the approved scope may require BACI approval and revised commercial terms.

5. TECHNOLOGY PARTNER ACCESS

Technology Partner access may include APIs, SDKs, integration services, data interfaces, sandbox access, Production access, technical documentation, partner support and other capabilities identified by BACI.

Actual entitlements depend on the applicable plan, Mode, Capacity, Order and integration approval.

6. LIMITED LICENCE

Subject to the Agreement, BACI grants Technology Partner a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable and non-sublicensable licence during the applicable term to use approved BACI Technology solely to develop, test, operate and support the approved integration.

Any sublicensing, embedding, white-labelling, distribution or End-Customer right must be expressly granted.

All rights not expressly granted are reserved by BACI.

7. NO IMPLIED RIGHTS

Access to documentation, APIs, schemas, sandbox environments, demonstrations or technical information does not grant ownership, resale, sublicensing, source-code, model-weight, trademark or other rights beyond those expressly stated.

No right arises by implication, estoppel or technical capability except where Applicable Law requires otherwise.

8. DEVELOPER & API TERMS

Technology Partner must comply with the Developer & API Terms for credentials, authentication, rate limits, webhooks, versioning, sandbox, Production use, security, prohibited technical conduct and related developer matters.

Where this Agreement expressly provides a different Technology Partner rule, this Agreement controls for that matter subject to the overall order of precedence.

9. SANDBOX

Sandbox access is for development, testing and evaluation unless BACI expressly authorises another purpose.

Technology Partner must not rely on Sandbox for live End-Customer operations.

Sandbox may have reduced functionality, Capacity, data, availability and support.

10. PRODUCTION APPROVAL

BACI may require technical, security, privacy, commercial and operational readiness before enabling Production integration.

Production approval does not certify Technology Partner's product, security or legal compliance.

Technology Partner remains responsible for its own Production deployment.

11. INTEGRATION DOCUMENTATION

Technology Partner must maintain accurate technical documentation sufficient to operate and support its side of the integration.

Customer-facing documentation must not misrepresent BACI functionality or responsibilities.

Technology Partner must update materially affected documentation when its integration changes.

12. CREDENTIALS

BACI credentials, secrets, tokens and certificates are confidential and must be protected according to the Developer & API Terms and reasonable security practice.

Technology Partner must not expose BACI credentials to End Customers unless BACI expressly designs and authorises the relevant delegated-access mechanism.

Suspected compromise must be reported promptly to security@bacihq.com.

13. AUTHENTICATION AND AUTHORISATION

Technology Partner must use BACI-supported authentication and authorisation mechanisms.

Technology Partner must request and use only permissions reasonably necessary for the approved integration.

Technical access does not create authority to access a Customer's data or take actions for that Customer without appropriate permission.

14. END-CUSTOMER AUTHORISATION

Where the integration accesses an End Customer's BACI account, organisation, data or connected systems, Technology Partner must obtain all permissions required by the applicable integration model and law.

Technology Partner must honour revocation and reduced permissions.

BACI may require direct End-Customer authorisation through BACI-controlled mechanisms.

15. END-CUSTOMER RELATIONSHIP

Technology Partner is independently responsible for its relationship with its End Customers, including its product terms, privacy disclosures, support, pricing, refunds and legal compliance.

BACI is not a party to Technology Partner's End-Customer contract merely because BACI functionality is integrated.

Where an End Customer separately contracts with BACI, that BACI agreement governs the End Customer's direct BACI rights.

16. END-CUSTOMER CAPACITY

Technology Partner plans may limit End-Customer organisations, integrations, workspaces, users, transactions, markets, usage or other Capacity.

Technology Partner must remain within contracted Capacity.

BACI may restrict additional activation beyond Capacity until an upgrade or additional entitlement is purchased.

17. MULTI-TENANT INTEGRATIONS

A multi-tenant Technology Partner must maintain logical separation appropriate to each End Customer and must not permit one End Customer to access another's data, credentials, Outputs or permissions.

Technology Partner must design tenant identification and authorisation controls appropriate to the integration.

BACI may require additional architecture review for material multi-tenant deployments.

18. EMBEDDED FUNCTIONALITY

Embedding BACI functionality within Technology Partner's product requires express commercial authorisation where the functionality is presented to End Customers.

The applicable Order should identify the approved embedded capabilities, attribution, access model and commercial basis.

Technology Partner may not expose substantially broader BACI functionality than authorised.

19. WHITE-LABEL RIGHTS

Technology Partner has no white-label right unless expressly stated in an Order or Platform & Intelligence Licensing Agreement.

Where white-labelling is authorised, Technology Partner must preserve required legal notices, data disclosures, intellectual-property protections and any agreed BACI attribution.

White-labelling does not transfer ownership of BACI Technology.

20. RESALE RIGHTS

Technology Partner may not resell BACI subscriptions, raw API access or standalone BACI functionality unless an executed agreement expressly grants resale rights.

Technology Partner may charge for its own product incorporating authorised BACI functionality where permitted.

Reseller economics, territories, discounts and customer ownership require express terms.

21. MARKETPLACE DISTRIBUTION

Distribution of an integration through a software, cloud or application marketplace may require BACI approval.

Technology Partner is responsible for marketplace listing accuracy, marketplace rules, its own merchant obligations and applicable fees.

A marketplace listing must not imply BACI certification or endorsement beyond BACI's actual approval.

22. INTEROPERABILITY

Technology Partner may use documented interfaces to create authorised interoperability.

Technology Partner must not circumvent access controls or use interoperability as a pretext to extract protected BACI Technology.

Nothing prevents lawful independent development using Technology Partner's own resources and information it has a right to use.

23. TECHNICAL LIMITS

Rate limits, quotas, concurrency limits, payload limits, Capacity and other technical restrictions apply as documented or contracted.

Technology Partner must not evade those controls through account splitting, credential rotation, distributed requests or other circumvention.

BACI may enforce limits to protect platform integrity and entitlements.

24. PERFORMANCE ENGINEERING

Technology Partner is responsible for designing its integration to handle documented latency, retries, timeouts, rate limits, partial failures and dependency outages.

Technology Partner should use idempotency, queuing, caching, fallback and circuit-breaking techniques where appropriate.

BACI does not guarantee that every integration pattern is suitable for Production.

25. WEBHOOKS AND EVENTS

Technology Partner must secure webhook endpoints, validate supported signatures or authentication, manage retries and duplicate delivery, and prevent replay where appropriate.

Technology Partner must not assume exactly-once delivery unless BACI expressly documents and contracts for it.

26. API VERSIONING

Technology Partner must maintain its integration against supported BACI API versions.

BACI may introduce new versions and retire older versions according to applicable lifecycle commitments.

Technology Partner must not rely on undocumented behaviour.

27. DEPRECATION

Where reasonably practicable, BACI will provide advance notice before retiring a materially used supported Production interface, except where security, law or third-party dependencies require faster action.

Any specific notice commitment in an Order or SLA controls.

Beta and preview interfaces may have shorter lifecycle expectations.

28. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

Each party should provide reasonable technical coordination for material integration changes within its control.

Technology Partner must test changes before Production deployment where practicable.

Neither party is required to preserve an undocumented dependency indefinitely.

29. INTEGRATION SECURITY

Technology Partner must maintain a security programme appropriate to the sensitivity and scale of the integration.

Controls should include secure development, access control, secret management, vulnerability management, logging, incident response and dependency management as appropriate.

BACI's security obligations remain those in the Security Addendum and Agreement.

30. SECURITY ASSESSMENT

BACI may require reasonable security information before approving or continuing a material integration, including questionnaires, architecture information, testing evidence or independent reports where proportionate.

BACI will treat non-public security materials as Confidential Information.

Technology Partner must not misrepresent its security posture or certifications.

31. VULNERABILITIES

Technology Partner must promptly remediate material vulnerabilities in its integration that threaten BACI, End Customers or protected data.

BACI may restrict affected functionality while a material vulnerability remains unresolved.

Good-faith vulnerability reporting to BACI should be directed to security@bacihq.com.

32. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Technology Partner must promptly notify BACI of a confirmed or reasonably suspected security incident that materially affects the integration, BACI credentials, BACI systems or BACI-related protected data.

The parties will reasonably cooperate on containment, investigation and remediation.

Legal notification responsibilities remain allocated by the applicable DPA, contracts and law.

33. DATA RIGHTS

Each party retains rights in data and technology it owns or controls, subject to licences expressly granted.

Technology Partner must have rights necessary for data it submits to BACI.

Access through an integration does not expand rights in Customer Data or Third-Party Data.

34. PERSONAL DATA

Where BACI Processes Customer Personal Data on behalf of a Customer, the DPA applies.

Technology Partner is responsible for determining its own privacy role and for lawful notices, permissions, agreements and rights applicable to its Processing.

The parties must not assume that integration makes them joint controllers unless the facts and law establish that role.

35. DATA MINIMISATION

Technology Partner should transmit only data reasonably necessary for the approved integration.

Secrets and unnecessary protected data should not be placed in logs, URLs, metadata or other unsuitable fields.

Technology Partner is responsible for its own logs and downstream copies.

36. DATA RESIDENCY AND TRANSFERS

BACI data residency and international transfer commitments are governed by the DPA, International Data Transfer Addendum and applicable Order.

Technology Partner is responsible for transfers created by its own systems, End Customers and subprocessors.

A regional endpoint does not create an unstated exclusive-residency commitment.

37. SUBPROCESSORS

BACI-selected Subprocessors are governed by the DPA and Subprocessor Policy & List.

Technology Partner is responsible for its own subprocessors, vendors and infrastructure.

A Technology Partner vendor does not become a BACI Subprocessor merely because it participates in Technology Partner's side of the integration.

38. DATA RETENTION AND DELETION

BACI retention and deletion are governed by the Data Retention & Deletion Policy, DPA and Agreement.

Technology Partner must establish its own lawful retention and deletion practices.

Deleting data in one party's environment does not automatically delete independently stored copies held by the other party.

39. CONFIDENTIALITY

Each party must protect the other's Confidential Information under the Terms or applicable negotiated confidentiality provisions.

Integration architecture, non-public APIs, security information, commercial terms, roadmaps and pre-release functionality may constitute Confidential Information.

Neither party may use Confidential Information outside the permitted relationship.

40. AI FUNCTIONALITY

Where the integration uses BACI AI Functionality, the AI & Autonomous Systems Terms apply.

Technology Partner is responsible for the way its product presents, contextualises and acts on AI Outputs.

Technology Partner must not remove material safeguards or disclosures required for lawful and responsible use.

41. NO GENERAL MODEL TRAINING BY DEFAULT

Unless the applicable Customer expressly agrees in writing or affirmatively opts into a clearly identified programme, BACI will not use Customer Confidential Information, Customer Personal Data, private Inputs or private Outputs to train or fine-tune general-purpose or shared AI models for the benefit of other customers.

Technology Partner integration does not weaken this protection.

The DPA remains authoritative for Customer Personal Data.

42. TECHNOLOGY PARTNER AI

Where Technology Partner supplies its own AI model, agent or automated system into the integration, Technology Partner is responsible for that technology, its training rights, instructions, safeguards, outputs and legal compliance.

BACI does not assume responsibility for Technology Partner AI merely because it interoperates with BACI.

43. AI AGENTS

Agentic integrations must operate within authenticated permissions, Authority Boundaries, Capacity and technical limits.

Technology Partner must not allow an agent to infer broader authority from possession of a token or API Credential.

Technical capability is not unlimited legal authority.

44. AUTONOMOUS ACTIONS

Where an integration initiates Autonomous Actions, the AI & Autonomous Systems Terms govern BACI functionality.

Technology Partner must preserve Customer-configured Authority Boundaries and must not bypass BACI approval or control mechanisms.

Autonomy does not remove governance.

45. HIGH-CONSEQUENCE USE

Technology Partner must not deploy the integration as the sole decision or operational control in a high-consequence context where law requires human review, specialised controls or prohibits the use.

BACI may require additional terms or controls for regulated or materially high-risk integrations.

46. SAFETY-CRITICAL SYSTEMS

Unless expressly authorised in a specialised agreement, the integration must not be used as the sole safety control for systems whose failure could directly cause death, serious bodily injury or catastrophic physical or environmental damage.

Technology Partner must implement independent safety measures appropriate to such systems.

47. ACCEPTABLE USE

Technology Partner and its authorised use of BACI are subject to the Acceptable Use Policy.

Technology Partner must not design an integration that systematically facilitates prohibited conduct.

BACI may restrict functionality reasonably associated with material abuse.

48. COMPLIANCE WITH LAW

Technology Partner must comply with laws applicable to its business, integration, data, End Customers and distribution.

BACI's approval of an integration is not legal advice or a representation that Technology Partner's deployment is lawful in every jurisdiction.

49. REGULATORY APPROVALS

Technology Partner is responsible for licences, registrations, certifications and approvals required for its product.

Technology Partner must not state that BACI's approval constitutes government or regulatory approval.

BACI may require evidence of authorisation for regulated deployments.

50. EXPORT CONTROLS AND SANCTIONS

Each party must comply with applicable export controls and sanctions.

Technology Partner must not use routing, End-Customer structures, intermediaries, VPNs or false information to evade restrictions.

BACI may restrict access where required by law.

51. ANTI-BRIBERY

Technology Partner must comply with applicable anti-bribery and anti-corruption laws.

Technology Partner must not offer improper value in connection with BACI, customers, procurement or public officials.

BACI may terminate participation for material corruption risk as permitted by the Agreement.

52. COMPETITION LAW

The parties must independently determine their commercial conduct and comply with competition law.

Nothing authorises price fixing, market allocation, bid rigging or unlawful exchange of competitively sensitive information.

Commercial collaboration must remain within the legitimate scope of the partnership.

53. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY — BACI

BACI retains all rights in BACI Technology, including APIs, models, algorithms, orchestration, schemas, taxonomies, scoring, documentation and improvements.

No ownership transfers to Technology Partner except as expressly stated.

Technology Partner must preserve applicable proprietary notices.

54. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY — PARTNER

Technology Partner retains rights in its independently developed technology, products, trademarks, documentation and materials.

BACI receives only the rights reasonably necessary to support, test, secure, demonstrate or operate the approved integration as expressly agreed.

BACI does not acquire Technology Partner's product merely because it connects to BACI.

55. JOINT DEVELOPMENT

Jointly developed technology, connectors, specifications or other deliverables require an Order or SOW that expressly allocates ownership and licence rights.

Neither party should assume joint ownership merely because personnel collaborated.

Pre-existing technology remains with its original owner unless expressly transferred.

56. FEEDBACK

Either party may voluntarily provide feedback.

Unless otherwise agreed, the receiving party may use non-confidential feedback without payment, provided that doing so does not transfer ownership of the other party's Confidential Information, data or independently developed technology.

57. BRAND LICENCE

Neither party may use the other's marks except under express written permission, applicable brand guidelines or an approved marketplace/integration listing.

Approved use is limited, revocable, non-exclusive and does not transfer ownership.

Marks must not be altered or used misleadingly.

58. PUBLIC ANNOUNCEMENTS

Neither party may issue a press release or public announcement naming the other as a Technology Partner without required approval, except where disclosure is legally required.

Approval of one announcement does not create standing approval for future publicity.

59. PARTNER DIRECTORY

Where BACI maintains a partner or integration directory, inclusion may be subject to BACI's then-current programme criteria.

BACI may remove an inaccurate, unsupported, insecure, discontinued or non-compliant listing.

Directory inclusion does not guarantee leads or revenue.

60. CO-MARKETING

Co-marketing activities require mutual approval of applicable content, claims, branding and campaign scope.

Each party is responsible for its own marketing-law compliance and approved statements.

Neither party may promise the other's roadmap or commercial terms.

61. LEADS AND OPPORTUNITIES

Unless a separate programme expressly provides otherwise, neither party is obligated to provide leads, referrals, exclusivity or minimum opportunity volume.

Customer and opportunity ownership must be determined by the applicable commercial arrangement.

Confidential lead information may be used only for the authorised purpose.

62. NON-EXCLUSIVITY

Unless an executed agreement expressly states otherwise, the relationship is non-exclusive.

BACI may work with competing technology providers and Technology Partner may work with competing platforms.

Confidentiality and intellectual-property obligations continue to apply.

63. COMMERCIAL MODEL

Technology Partner Fees are determined by the applicable Order and may reflect access category, Mode, Capacity, usage, integrations, End-Customer organisations, support, deployment or other contracted dimensions.

Technology Partner access does not automatically include unlimited usage.

The Billing Policy applies.

64. USAGE CHARGES

API, compute, model, monitoring, generation, execution, storage or other usage may be metered where disclosed or contracted.

Technology Partner is responsible for billable activity generated through credentials and systems within its control, subject to the Agreement's allocation for unauthorised activity and BACI-controlled failures.

65. END-CUSTOMER BILLING

Where Technology Partner bills its own End Customers, Technology Partner is responsible for its invoices, taxes, refunds and collections.

BACI is not responsible for End-Customer non-payment to Technology Partner.

Where BACI bills an End Customer directly, BACI's agreement with that End Customer governs the BACI charge.

66. TAXES

Taxes are allocated under the applicable Order, Billing Policy and law.

Each party remains responsible for taxes arising from its own income, payroll and independent business activities.

Neither party provides the other tax advice.

67. SUPPORT MODEL

The applicable Order should identify the support model for the integration.

Technology Partner ordinarily remains responsible for first-line support concerning its own product, while BACI supports BACI-controlled Services according to the applicable plan or SLA.

The parties should cooperate on issues crossing the integration boundary.

68. ESCALATION

For material integration incidents, the parties may use technical and business escalation contacts identified in the Order or operational documentation.

Escalation does not alter contractual responsibility.

Each party should provide information reasonably necessary to diagnose the portion of the issue within its control.

69. SERVICE LEVELS

Binding BACI service levels exist only where an SLA or Order expressly covers the relevant Service.

Technology Partner must not promise an End Customer a stronger BACI SLA than BACI has contractually provided unless Technology Partner independently assumes that obligation.

SLA credits are governed by the SLA.

70. BUSINESS CONTINUITY

Each party is responsible for continuity measures for its own systems.

Technology Partner should design resilient integration behaviour appropriate to business consequence.

BACI's continuity commitments are limited to those in the Agreement and Security Addendum.

71. INSURANCE

Each party is responsible for insurance required by law and its contractual obligations.

BACI may require Technology Partner to maintain specified cyber, technology errors-and-omissions, commercial general liability or other insurance for particular high-scale or high-risk relationships where stated in an Order.

Any required limits should be expressly documented.

72. RECORDS

Each party may maintain records reasonably necessary for operation, billing, security, compliance and support.

Technology Partner must maintain records sufficient to substantiate material End-Customer authorisation and compliance where relevant.

Retention remains subject to applicable privacy and legal requirements.

73. COMPLIANCE REVIEW

BACI may request reasonable information to verify Technology Partner's compliance with approved scope, Capacity, security, branding and commercial rights.

BACI will not use this right to obtain unrelated trade secrets without legitimate need.

Formal audit rights in a negotiated agreement control where applicable.

74. SUBCONTRACTORS

Technology Partner may use subcontractors subject to its obligations to End Customers, Applicable Law and any express BACI restrictions.

Technology Partner remains responsible for its subcontractors to the extent provided by the Agreement and law.

Subcontracting does not expand Technology Partner's BACI licence.

75. CHANGE OF CONTROL

Technology Partner must provide notice of a change of control where required by an Order or where the change materially affects sanctions, security, competitive conflict or contractual eligibility.

Assignment and change-of-control rights are governed by the Terms and applicable negotiated agreement.

76. SUSPENSION

BACI may suspend Technology Partner access for material breach, security risk, prohibited use, non-payment, legal requirement or other grounds permitted by the Terms.

Where reasonably practicable, BACI will seek to limit suspension to the affected integration, credential or functionality.

Suspension does not eliminate accrued Fees.

77. EMERGENCY TECHNICAL ACTION

BACI may immediately revoke credentials, disable an endpoint, isolate an integration or impose protective limits where reasonably necessary to contain a material security or platform threat.

BACI will restore access when the material risk is resolved where the Agreement otherwise remains in force.

78. TERM

This Agreement begins when accepted or incorporated and continues while Technology Partner has an applicable Technology Partner relationship, unless terminated earlier.

Individual Orders may have fixed terms and renewal provisions.

79. NON-RENEWAL

Non-renewal is governed by the applicable Order and Cancellation Policy.

Technology Partner must comply with any negotiated advance notice requirement.

Non-renewal does not erase obligations accrued during the term.

80. TERMINATION FOR CAUSE

Termination for material breach is governed by the Terms and applicable negotiated agreement.

Where a cure period applies, the terminating party must follow it unless immediate termination is permitted.

Refund consequences are governed by the Refund Policy and Agreement.

81. EFFECT OF TERMINATION

On termination, Technology Partner must stop using BACI Technology except for surviving rights, cease new BACI-dependent activity and discontinue misleading claims of active partnership.

BACI may revoke credentials and integration access.

Accrued Fees and provisions intended to survive remain enforceable.

82. END-CUSTOMER TRANSITION

Technology Partner is responsible for planning continuity for End Customers affected by termination.

BACI is not obligated to continue Technology Partner access solely because Technology Partner has downstream commitments unless an Order expressly provides transition rights.

BACI and Technology Partner may agree an orderly transition where commercially and technically feasible.

83. DATA RETURN AND DELETION

Data return, retention and deletion are governed by the DPA, Terms and Data Retention & Deletion Policy.

Technology Partner must separately address data in its own systems.

Termination does not automatically delete independently held copies.

84. WARRANTIES

Each party's warranties are those stated in the Agreement and applicable Order.

Technology Partner warrants that it has authority to enter this Agreement, will comply with Applicable Law and will not knowingly introduce malicious code into the integration.

BACI does not warrant Technology Partner's product or End-Customer services.

85. INDEMNITIES

Indemnity obligations are governed by the Terms and any negotiated agreement.

Without duplicative recovery, Technology Partner remains responsible to the extent allocated by the Agreement for third-party claims arising from Technology Partner technology, unlawful instructions, lack of End-Customer authority, Technology Partner materials or Technology Partner's breach.

86. LIABILITY

Liability between BACI and Technology Partner is governed by the Terms, including applicable exclusions, caps, enhanced caps and non-excludable liabilities.

Neither party assumes the other's independent liability to End Customers merely because the products integrate.

Mandatory Applicable Law remains unaffected.

87. NO THIRD-PARTY BENEFICIARIES BY DEFAULT

End Customers do not become third-party beneficiaries of this Agreement solely because they use Technology Partner's integrated product, except where an Order or mandatory law expressly provides otherwise.

Direct BACI customers retain rights under their own BACI agreements.

88. INDEPENDENT CONTRACTORS

The parties are independent contractors.

Nothing creates a partnership, joint venture, franchise, employment, fiduciary or general agency relationship.

Neither party may bind the other without express written authority.

89. NO MINIMUM BUSINESS COMMITMENT

Unless an Order expressly states otherwise, neither party guarantees minimum revenue, transactions, End Customers, leads, integrations or commercial success.

Forecasts and pipeline discussions are not binding commitments unless incorporated into an executed agreement.

90. CHANGES TO THIS AGREEMENT

BACI may update this Agreement prospectively in accordance with the Terms.

A lower-precedence update will not override an expressly negotiated higher-precedence provision where the order of precedence prevents that result.

Mandatory notice requirements will be followed.

91. INTERPRETATION

Technology Partner status grants only the integration and commercial rights expressly authorised.

Technical interoperability does not equal resale authority; API access does not equal white-label authority; and End-Customer access does not make BACI a party to Technology Partner's customer contract.

Mandatory Applicable Law controls to the required extent.

92. CONTACT

Technology Partner enquiries: access@bacihq.com

Licensing enquiries: licensing@bacihq.com

Billing: billing@bacihq.com

Privacy: privacy@bacihq.com

Security: security@bacihq.com

ANNEX I — TECHNOLOGY PARTNER RESPONSIBILITY MATRIX

BACI is responsible for BACI-controlled Services; BACI credentials and authentication mechanisms within BACI's control; BACI-selected Subprocessor governance; BACI security and data-use commitments; and BACI support according to the applicable Order.

Technology Partner is responsible for its product; integration code; End-Customer relationships; its own infrastructure; its credentials and secrets; lawful permissions; its subprocessors; its support obligations; and the security and compliance of its side of the integration.

Responsibility may be shared for integration testing, incident coordination, data mapping, deployment validation and cross-system troubleshooting.

The applicable Agreement controls where responsibility is expressly allocated differently.

ANNEX II — PRODUCTION APPROVAL CHECKLIST

Before Production activation, BACI may assess:

1. approved use case and commercial scope; 2. technical architecture; 3. authentication and authorisation; 4. tenant isolation; 5. credential management; 6. data flows and privacy roles; 7. international transfers; 8. security controls; 9. vulnerability management; 10. logging and monitoring; 11. rate and Capacity planning; 12. retry and idempotency behaviour; 13. webhook security; 14. support model; 15. incident escalation; 16. AI and Autonomous Authority Boundaries where applicable; 17. End-Customer permissions; 18. marketplace or embedded distribution; 19. branding and attribution; and 20. compliance with the AUP and applicable law.

Production approval does not transfer Technology Partner's responsibility for its own product.

ANNEX III — COMMERCIAL RIGHTS MATRIX

STANDARD TECHNOLOGY PARTNER RIGHTS — approved integration development; Sandbox and authorised Production use; documented APIs; agreed End-Customer integration; technical support according to plan; and approved interoperability.

REQUIRES EXPRESS ORDER LANGUAGE — resale; white-labelling; sublicensing; embedded BACI access offered as a standalone feature; BACI-branded marketplace distribution; joint selling; revenue share; exclusivity; territory rights; source-code access; platform licensing; or rights to redistribute BACI Intelligence beyond ordinary authorised Outputs.

NOT GRANTED — ownership of BACI Technology; authority to bind BACI; unrestricted credential sharing; unlimited Capacity; undocumented API rights; or rights to circumvent BACI controls.

ANNEX IV — INTEGRATION SECURITY BASELINE

Technology Partner should implement, as appropriate:

secure software development; • least-privilege access; • separate Sandbox and Production credentials; • secret-management controls; • encryption in transit; • secure webhook validation; • replay and duplicate-event controls; • idempotency for consequential actions; • vulnerability and dependency management; • tenant isolation; • logging and anomaly monitoring; • incident-response procedures; • credential rotation and rapid revocation; • rate and concurrency controls; • data minimisation; • secure deletion; • AI prompt-injection and tool-abuse controls where relevant; • change management; • business continuity; and • periodic security review.

Controls should be proportionate to integration risk and data sensitivity.

ANNEX V — END-CUSTOMER GOVERNANCE

For integrations serving End Customers, Technology Partner should maintain:

accurate End-Customer identity; • valid End-Customer authorisation; • tenant separation; • appropriate data-processing terms; • clear privacy disclosures; • permissions and scopes; • revocation mechanisms; • support ownership; • billing responsibility; • applicable AI disclosures; • Authority Boundaries for Autonomous functionality; • retention and deletion processes; • incident-notification procedures; • offboarding controls; and • a process for ending BACI access when the End Customer relationship ends.

Technology Partner must not use End-Customer data after authority ends except where lawfully permitted.
