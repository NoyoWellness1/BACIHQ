SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

Version 1.0 · Effective Date: 11 September 2026 · Last Updated: 11 September 2026

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") forms part of the BACI Legal Framework and applies only where an Order expressly includes a paid BACI service level. It defines BACI's availability commitment, support severity framework, service-credit remedy, measurement rules and exclusions for the covered Production Services. Capitalised terms not defined here have the meanings in the applicable BACI Terms of Service, Enterprise Customer Agreement / Master Services Agreement or Order.

1. SCOPE

This SLA applies to the generally available BACI Production Services expressly identified as SLA-covered in Customer's Order. It does not apply to free, trial, preview, beta, sandbox, evaluation or non-production functionality unless the Order expressly states otherwise.

2. CONTRACTUAL STATUS

This SLA creates binding service-level commitments only for a Customer whose Order incorporates it. Public status pages, marketing statements, estimates, roadmaps and operational targets do not independently create an SLA.

3. PRECEDENCE

The BACI order of precedence applies. This SLA controls service-level commitments, measurement, exclusions and service-credit remedies. An executed Order may state a different service level or remedy for the expressly identified Service.

4. DEFINITIONS

“Available” means the covered Production Service is capable of accepting and processing valid Customer requests for its material contracted core functionality, subject to the exclusions in this SLA.

“Downtime” means a period during which the covered Production Service is Unavailable.

“Monthly Uptime Percentage” or “MUP” means the percentage of total minutes in a calendar month during which the covered Production Service was Available, calculated under this SLA.

“Production Service” means generally available paid BACI functionality designated for production use.

“Service Credit” means a credit calculated under this SLA against eligible recurring Fees for the affected covered Service.

5. STANDARD AVAILABILITY COMMITMENT

Unless Customer's Order states a different commitment, BACI will use commercially reasonable measures to provide a Monthly Uptime Percentage of at least 99.9% for the covered Production Service.

6. MONTHLY UPTIME CALCULATION

Monthly Uptime Percentage = ((Total Minutes in the Month - Eligible Downtime Minutes) / Total Minutes in the Month) × 100. Excluded Events are not counted as Eligible Downtime. Measurement is based on BACI's service telemetry and records, subject to correction of manifest error.

7. SERVICE-CREDIT SCHEDULE

If BACI fails to meet the Standard Availability Commitment for a covered Production Service during a calendar month, an eligible Customer may claim the following Service Credit against the monthly recurring Fees allocable to the affected covered Service.

8. CREDIT BASE

The Service Credit is calculated only against the recurring subscription Fees allocable to the affected covered Production Service for the affected calendar month. Usage charges, taxes, Professional Services, implementation Fees, third-party charges, one-time Fees and unrelated Services are excluded unless the Order expressly provides otherwise.

9. MAXIMUM MONTHLY CREDIT

Total Service Credits for all availability failures affecting the same covered Production Service in a calendar month will not exceed 50% of the eligible recurring monthly Fees for that Service.

10. ANNUAL AND PREPAID CUSTOMERS

For an annual or other prepaid subscription, the monthly credit base is the pro rata monthly portion of the recurring subscription Fees allocable to the affected covered Service, unless the Order states another calculation.

11. SERVICE CREDITS ARE NOT CASH

Service Credits are applied to future BACI invoices or account balances and have no cash value except where applicable law or an express Order requires otherwise. Credits cannot exceed Fees actually paid or payable for the eligible credit base.

12. EXCLUSIVE SLA REMEDY

Except where an Order expressly provides otherwise, a Service Credit is Customer's sole and exclusive monetary remedy for BACI's failure to meet the availability commitment in this SLA. This does not limit termination rights for uncured material breach, liability for matters outside contractual limitations, or rights that cannot lawfully be restricted.

13. CREDIT CLAIM REQUIREMENT

Customer must submit a Service Credit request to billing@bacihq.com within 30 days after the end of the calendar month in which the qualifying availability failure occurred. The request should identify Customer, affected organisation, affected Service, approximate dates and times, and relevant incident or support references.

14. VALIDATION

BACI will reasonably validate a claim against service telemetry, incident records and applicable exclusions. BACI may request information reasonably necessary to identify the affected Service or Customer experience. BACI will not reject an otherwise valid claim solely because Customer cannot provide telemetry controlled exclusively by BACI.

15. CREDIT APPLICATION

Approved Service Credits will ordinarily be applied to a subsequent invoice or account balance. If the affected subscription has ended and no future invoice will issue, treatment will follow the Agreement and applicable law.

16. NO AUTOMATIC MULTIPLICATION

A single event that affects multiple SLA metrics does not create duplicative credits for the same Fees unless the Order expressly provides otherwise. The highest applicable credit for the affected Service and period applies, subject to the maximum monthly credit.

17. COVERED SERVICE BOUNDARY

Availability is measured at the BACI-controlled service boundary for the covered Production Service. Customer-side networks, devices, browsers, identity providers, Customer systems and third-party services outside BACI's control are not part of the measured BACI service boundary.

18. PARTIAL FUNCTIONALITY

A degradation is Downtime only where it materially prevents the affected covered Production Service from performing its contracted core functionality for Customer. Minor defects, cosmetic issues, individual feature impairment or reduced performance that does not materially prevent core use is not automatically Downtime.

19. REGIONAL OR COMPONENT FAILURE

Where a Service is architected by region, environment or material component, Downtime may be measured for the affected region, environment or component and the Customers actually affected. An isolated failure does not create a global outage for unaffected Customers.

20. API AVAILABILITY

Where an Order includes a covered Production API, API availability is measured by BACI's ability to receive and materially process valid authenticated requests at the supported BACI API boundary, excluding Customer code, invalid requests, rate-limit violations and external dependencies.

21. AUTONOMOUS SERVICE AVAILABILITY

For covered Autonomous functionality, availability means the BACI-controlled execution service is materially capable of processing authorised actions. A Customer approval hold, Authority Boundary, spending limit, exhausted Capacity, disabled integration or third-party refusal is not BACI Downtime.

22. INTELLIGENCE DATA FRESHNESS

Availability does not by itself guarantee that every intelligence source, external dataset or third-party feed is refreshed continuously or at a particular interval. Any binding freshness commitment must be expressly stated in the Order or a product-specific schedule.

23. FORECAST AND MODEL OUTPUT

The SLA measures service availability, not the accuracy, commercial success or correctness of an AI prediction, forecast, recommendation, classification or Output. AI quality obligations are governed by the Terms and AI & Autonomous Systems Terms.

24. EXCLUDED EVENTS

The following are excluded from Eligible Downtime to the extent they cause or materially contribute to unavailability: scheduled maintenance within the maintenance framework; emergency maintenance reasonably necessary to protect security or stability; Customer systems or Customer-controlled configuration; Customer breach or prohibited use; Customer credentials or identity provider failure; third-party services outside BACI's reasonable control; internet or telecommunications failures outside BACI's service boundary; force majeure; legal or regulatory action requiring suspension; Customer-requested suspension; exhausted contracted Capacity; beta, preview, sandbox or trial functionality; and actions BACI reasonably takes to prevent or contain a material security threat.

25. CUSTOMER-CAUSED EVENTS

Downtime caused by Customer code, integrations, malformed requests, unsupported configuration, unauthorised modifications, Customer Data, Customer infrastructure, Customer-selected third parties or failure to follow material Documentation is excluded to the extent of the causal impact.

26. THIRD-PARTY DEPENDENCIES

A failure of a Third-Party Service is excluded where it is outside BACI's reasonable control and BACI has not expressly assumed the third party's availability obligation. BACI remains responsible for BACI-controlled architecture and for obligations it expressly undertakes concerning its selected Subprocessors.

27. SUBPROCESSOR FAILURES

Use of a Subprocessor does not automatically excuse BACI from this SLA. If a BACI-selected Subprocessor failure causes the covered BACI Production Service itself to be Unavailable, the event is treated according to this SLA unless the failure qualifies under another express exclusion.

28. FORCE MAJEURE

Force majeure is governed by the Agreement. BACI will use commercially reasonable efforts to mitigate service impact and restore covered functionality where reasonably possible.

29. SCHEDULED MAINTENANCE

BACI may perform scheduled maintenance reasonably necessary to maintain, improve or secure the Services. Where scheduled maintenance is expected materially to affect Production availability, BACI will use commercially reasonable efforts to provide advance notice through an appropriate Customer communication or service-status channel.

30. MAINTENANCE DESIGN

BACI will use commercially reasonable engineering practices to minimise material disruption from planned maintenance. No fixed maintenance window is created unless stated in an Order or product schedule.

31. EMERGENCY MAINTENANCE

BACI may perform emergency maintenance without ordinary advance notice where reasonably necessary to address a material security vulnerability, active threat, legal requirement, data-integrity risk or serious stability issue. BACI will communicate material Customer impact as soon as reasonably practicable.

32. STATUS COMMUNICATIONS

For a material Production incident, BACI will use reasonable efforts to provide affected Customers with appropriate status information through available support, service-status or designated communication channels.

33. INCIDENT UPDATES

The frequency and content of incident updates will be proportionate to severity and available verified information. BACI will not be required to publish speculative root-cause information while an incident is under active investigation.

34. ROOT CAUSE ANALYSIS

For a Severity 1 incident materially affecting an Enterprise Customer, BACI will provide a reasonable post-incident summary upon request where appropriate. A detailed root-cause analysis is provided where the applicable support plan or Order includes it.

35. SECURITY INCIDENTS

Security Incident notification is governed by the DPA and Security Addendum, not by the general incident-update cadence in this SLA. Security communications may be limited where disclosure would create additional risk or violate law.

36. SUPPORT SCOPE

Support service levels apply only to Customers whose Order includes the relevant paid support level. Support response targets are measured from BACI's receipt of a sufficiently detailed support request through the designated support channel.

37. SEVERITY 1 - CRITICAL

Severity 1 means a verified Production incident causing complete or near-complete loss of a material covered Service for a substantial portion of Customer's authorised production users, or a critical production condition with no reasonable workaround and material business impact.

38. SEVERITY 2 - HIGH

Severity 2 means a significant degradation or loss of important Production functionality with material business impact, but where the Service remains partially usable or a reasonable workaround exists.

39. SEVERITY 3 - STANDARD

Severity 3 means a non-critical defect, limited functional issue or support problem that does not materially prevent normal business operations.

40. SEVERITY 4 - GENERAL

Severity 4 means a general question, configuration inquiry, documentation request, feature guidance or low-impact issue.

41. STANDARD ENTERPRISE RESPONSE TARGETS

Unless Customer's Order states a different support plan, the following initial-response targets apply to an Enterprise Order that expressly includes this SLA.

42. RESPONSE IS NOT RESOLUTION

An Initial Response Target is a target for BACI to acknowledge, triage or begin substantive handling of the issue. It is not a guaranteed resolution time unless an Order expressly states a resolution commitment.

43. SUPPORT CLOCK

For targets stated in business hours or business days, time runs only during the applicable BACI support hours stated in the Order or support documentation. Severity 1 coverage runs continuously only for Enterprise Orders that include 24×7 Critical support.

44. CUSTOMER AVAILABILITY

Customer must provide reasonably requested diagnostic information and maintain an available technical contact for Severity 1 incidents. Time during which BACI is materially blocked awaiting Customer information, access, approval or testing may be excluded from support-response or restoration calculations to the extent of the blockage.

45. SEVERITY CLASSIFICATION

Customer should select severity in good faith. BACI may reasonably reclassify a ticket where the reported facts do not satisfy the stated severity definition and will communicate the basis for a material reclassification.

46. ESCALATION

Enterprise Customers may request escalation of a material unresolved support issue through the escalation process identified in their Order or support documentation.

47. TARGET RESTORATION

BACI will prioritise restoration of material Production functionality over permanent correction where appropriate. A workaround, rollback, failover or temporary mitigation may constitute restoration while permanent remediation continues.

48. PROBLEM MANAGEMENT

BACI may group related incidents under a common problem record and address an underlying cause through a coordinated remediation. This does not reduce valid Service Credit rights.

49. SUPPORT EXCLUSIONS

Support targets do not apply to requests outside the purchased Service, unsupported custom code, general consulting, Professional Services deliverables, Customer training, feature requests, third-party product support or issues BACI cannot reproduce because Customer does not provide reasonably necessary information.

50. SERVICE REQUESTS

Administrative service requests, configuration changes and non-incident requests may have separate fulfilment targets stated in the Order or Documentation. They are not Availability incidents.

51. PROFESSIONAL SERVICES

Project milestones, SOW dates and Professional Services delivery are governed by the applicable SOW. They are not measured under this SLA unless the SOW expressly incorporates a service-level metric.

52. RECOVERY OBJECTIVES

Any binding Recovery Time Objective, Recovery Point Objective, disaster-recovery tier or continuity metric must be expressly stated in the Order, Security Addendum or product-specific schedule. This SLA does not invent an unstated RTO or RPO.

53. BACKUP RESTORATION

Backup and restoration obligations are governed by the Security Addendum, Data Retention & Deletion Policy and applicable product design. A Customer-specific restoration commitment exists only where expressly contracted.

54. DISASTER RECOVERY TESTING

BACI will maintain continuity testing appropriate to its contracted security obligations. Customer-specific participation, reports or recovery exercises require an express agreement.

55. CUSTOMER CONTINUITY

Customer is responsible for assessing whether the contracted Service Level meets its business requirements and for maintaining appropriate Customer-controlled continuity, backup, approval and fallback procedures.

56. MONITORING

BACI may use automated monitoring, telemetry, synthetic testing, infrastructure metrics, application metrics and incident records to determine availability and diagnose service health.

57. TIME STANDARD

Unless otherwise stated, availability measurement uses a consistent BACI system time standard. Calendar-month calculations are not altered by Customer's local time zone.

58. ROUNDING

Monthly Uptime Percentage may be calculated to sufficient precision to determine the applicable credit tier. BACI will not use rounding to avoid an otherwise qualifying Service Credit.

59. MULTI-TENANT INCIDENTS

A shared incident may affect Customers differently. Credit eligibility is determined by the material availability impact on the affected Customer's covered Service, not merely by the existence of a platform incident.

60. MULTI-REGION CUSTOMERS

Where Customer uses multiple independent regions or environments, availability may be measured separately where the architecture and Order reasonably support separate measurement.

61. CUSTOMER TEST ENVIRONMENTS

Development, test, staging, sandbox and other non-production environments are excluded unless expressly designated as SLA-covered in the Order.

62. MOBILE APPLICATIONS

A mobile application store outage, device operating-system issue, mobile carrier failure or Customer device problem is outside the BACI Production Service boundary. BACI-controlled backend availability remains measured where covered.

63. EMAIL AND NOTIFICATION DELIVERY

Where BACI sends email, SMS, push or other notifications through external networks or providers, successful handoff from BACI does not guarantee final delivery by the external provider unless expressly contracted.

64. EXTERNAL DATA SOURCES

Temporary unavailability, latency or changes in a third-party public or licensed data source do not constitute BACI Downtime unless the Order expressly makes that source availability part of the covered Service Level. BACI remains responsible for BACI-controlled processing.

65. RATE LIMITS

Enforcement of documented or contracted rate limits, concurrency limits or Capacity is not Downtime. A BACI error that materially prevents use below applicable limits may qualify if the other SLA conditions are met.

66. SUSPENSION

A suspension validly imposed under the Agreement for non-payment, security, legal requirement, prohibited use or Customer breach is excluded from Downtime for the affected period.

67. SERVICE CREDIT ELIGIBILITY

Customer must be current on undisputed payment obligations for the affected Service when the credit is applied. BACI will not withhold a valid credit merely because an unrelated amount is disputed in good faith.

68. NO CREDIT FOR FREE SERVICE

No Service Credit is payable for a Service for which Customer paid no recurring Fee during the affected period, unless the Order expressly establishes another remedy.

69. RESELLER OR PARTNER PURCHASES

Where Customer purchases through an authorised reseller or partner, BACI may issue an approved Service Credit through the applicable commercial channel. The credit cannot exceed the eligible amount attributable to the affected BACI Service.

70. GOVERNMENT CUSTOMERS

Government and Public Sector Customers are subject to the Government & Public Sector Schedule. A government-specific SLA, remedy or statutory requirement applies only where incorporated or mandatory.

71. REGIONAL RIGHTS

Mandatory local rights remain unaffected. Where applicable law requires a remedy beyond this SLA, that law controls to the extent it cannot be waived.

72. MATERIAL REPEATED FAILURE

Repeated material failure to meet the availability commitment may constitute a material breach where the facts and governing Agreement support that conclusion. Service Credits do not eliminate an express termination right for uncured material breach.

73. CHRONIC SERVICE FAILURE

An Enterprise Order may define a Chronic Service Failure threshold and associated termination right. No special threshold is implied unless stated in the Order.

74. NO BUSINESS-OUTCOME SLA

This SLA does not guarantee revenue, conversion, procurement success, grant success, investment, forecast accuracy, campaign performance, decision quality or another commercial outcome.

75. NO AI-ACCURACY SLA

Availability of AI functionality does not constitute a warranty that a model Output is correct. Any expressly contracted model-performance metric must be stated in a product-specific schedule and measured separately from uptime.

76. NO THIRD-PARTY SLA PASS-THROUGH

BACI does not automatically pass through a third party's SLA or credit to Customer, and Customer does not automatically receive a third party's contractual remedy. BACI's obligation is the SLA BACI expressly provides.

77. SECURITY PROTECTION

BACI may take immediate action reasonably necessary to protect Customer Data, the Services or other customers. Properly taken emergency security action is an Excluded Event to the extent necessary, but BACI remains responsible for its independent contractual security obligations.

78. CHANGE MANAGEMENT

BACI will use reasonable change-management practices for Production Services. An unsuccessful BACI-controlled Production change that causes eligible unavailability is not excluded merely because it was a change activity, unless it qualifies as scheduled or emergency maintenance under this SLA.

79. CAPACITY MANAGEMENT

BACI will manage BACI-controlled service capacity consistent with the contracted Service. Customer remains responsible for purchased Capacity and for promptly addressing Customer-specific usage that exceeds contracted limits.

80. SERVICE DEPENDENCY DESIGN

BACI may redesign internal components, infrastructure, models or providers while maintaining the contracted Service Level and other Agreement obligations. The SLA applies to the Customer-facing covered Service rather than requiring a particular internal architecture.

81. SERVICE STATUS RECORDS

BACI may maintain internal or public service-status records. A public status entry is informative and does not by itself determine individual Customer credit eligibility.

82. CUSTOMER MONITORING

Customer may maintain its own reasonable monitoring. If Customer evidence materially conflicts with BACI telemetry, the parties will compare available evidence in good faith rather than treating either source as automatically conclusive.

83. AUDIT OF SLA CALCULATION

Upon a reasonable, specific dispute concerning an SLA calculation, BACI will provide sufficient information to explain the calculation, subject to confidentiality, security and protection of other customers. This does not create a general source-code or infrastructure audit right.

84. FRAUDULENT CLAIMS

BACI may reject claims based on fabricated telemetry, manipulated requests, deliberate outage creation or other abuse. Intentional interference with the Service to manufacture a credit may violate the Acceptable Use Policy.

85. CREDIT TAX TREATMENT

Service Credits are billing adjustments. Tax treatment will follow applicable law and BACI's invoicing requirements.

86. TRANSFER OF CREDITS

Service Credits are non-transferable except as part of a permitted assignment of the underlying Agreement or where BACI expressly agrees.

87. EXPIRY OF CREDITS

An approved Service Credit will remain available for application according to the billing system and Agreement. BACI will not impose an undisclosed expiry that defeats a timely approved credit.

88. TERMINATED ACCOUNTS

If an eligible credit is approved after termination, BACI may apply it against outstanding eligible amounts or handle it according to the Agreement and applicable law.

89. SUPPORT LANGUAGE

Support may be provided in languages BACI makes available. No particular language commitment is created unless stated in the Order.

90. NAMED SUPPORT CONTACTS

BACI may require Enterprise Customers to designate authorised support contacts for security and efficient incident handling. Customer is responsible for keeping those contacts current.

91. REMOTE ACCESS

Where troubleshooting requires BACI access to a Customer-controlled system, Customer must expressly authorise appropriate access. BACI will use such access only for authorised support purposes.

92. LOGS AND DIAGNOSTICS

Customer authorises BACI to Process relevant service logs, diagnostics and technical metadata as permitted by the Agreement to investigate incidents, secure the Service and validate SLA performance.

93. CONFIDENTIAL INCIDENT INFORMATION

Non-public incident details, security information and root-cause materials may be BACI Confidential Information. Customer may use them for internal risk, compliance and operational purposes subject to the Agreement.

94. PUBLIC COMMUNICATIONS

Neither party will make misleading public statements concerning an incident or SLA performance. Where appropriate, the parties may coordinate communications without restricting legally required disclosures.

95. CHANGES TO THIS SLA

BACI may update this SLA prospectively according to the Agreement. A change will not retroactively reduce an already accrued Service Credit or override a higher-precedence negotiated SLA commitment.

96. SERVICE-SPECIFIC SCHEDULES

A product-specific SLA schedule may establish a different availability metric, support target, credit schedule, dependency model or exclusion for a particular Service. That schedule controls for the expressly identified Service.

97. ENTERPRISE NEGOTIATED SLA

Enterprise Customers may negotiate enhanced availability, support, recovery, incident-management or credit commitments in an Order or amendment. Enhanced commitments apply only to the expressly identified Services, environments and Fees.

98. INTERPRETATION

This SLA is intended to measure BACI-controlled service availability and support performance, not events outside BACI's reasonable control or Customer's commercial results. Exclusions will be interpreted according to their actual causal contribution and not more broadly than reasonably necessary.

99. CONTACT

Service and support incidents: use the BACI support channel designated in Customer's Order or Service interface.

Service Credit claims and billing: billing@bacihq.com

Security incidents: security@bacihq.com

Privacy: privacy@bacihq.com

Enterprise commercial matters: access@bacihq.com
