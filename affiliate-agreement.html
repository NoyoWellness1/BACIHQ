AFFILIATE PROGRAMME AGREEMENT

Version 1.0 · Effective Date: 10 September 2026 · Last Updated: 10 September 2026

This Affiliate Programme Agreement ("Agreement") governs participation in the BACI Affiliate Programme by an approved individual or organisation ("Affiliate").

The Programme permits eligible Affiliates to introduce qualifying customers to BACI and earn Commission on qualifying transactions under the rules below. It does not appoint Affiliate as BACI's sales agent, reseller, distributor, employee, franchisee, fiduciary or legal representative.

BACI's Programme is designed to reward genuine, attributable customer acquisition while protecting customers, BACI, and other participants from duplicate claims, self-referrals, misleading promotion, artificial attribution, fraud and other abuse.

1. INCORPORATION AND PRECEDENCE

This Agreement forms part of the BACI Legal Framework.

The Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Acceptable Use Policy and other applicable BACI terms are incorporated where relevant. If Affiliate separately purchases BACI Services, that customer relationship remains governed by the applicable BACI customer agreement.

Any expressly executed affiliate amendment or programme schedule controls where the BACI order of precedence gives it priority.

2. PROGRAMME ELIGIBILITY

BACI may accept individuals or organisations whose business, audience, content, professional standing, promotional methods and compliance posture are suitable for the Programme.

Participation is subject to BACI approval and continuing compliance.

BACI may decline an application where participation would create material legal, reputational, fraud, sanctions, brand or commercial risk.

3. APPLICATION INFORMATION

Affiliate must provide accurate, complete and current application, tax, payment and identity information.

Affiliate must not conceal beneficial ownership, location, promotional methods or other material information requested for compliance purposes.

Material changes must be updated promptly.

4. AUTHORITY

If Affiliate is an organisation, the person accepting this Agreement represents that they have authority to bind that organisation.

Only the approved Affiliate entity is entitled to Programme payments unless BACI expressly agrees otherwise.

Affiliate may not transfer its Programme account to another person or entity without BACI's approval.

5. DEFINITIONS

“Active Referred Customer” means a qualifying Referred Customer whose eligible BACI subscription remains active and paid for the period relevant to Commission.

“Affiliate Link” means a BACI-approved tracking link, code or other attribution mechanism assigned to Affiliate.

“Commission” means compensation payable for a Qualifying Transaction under this Agreement.

“Qualified Introduction” means a BACI-approved introduction meeting the requirements for the qualified-introduction programme described below.

“Qualifying Transaction” means an eligible paid transaction properly attributed to Affiliate and not excluded under this Agreement.

“Referred Customer” means an eligible new BACI customer attributed to Affiliate.

Other capitalised terms have the meanings in the BACI Legal Framework.

6. NON-EXCLUSIVITY

The Programme is non-exclusive.

BACI may operate other affiliate, referral, agency, adviser, reseller, Technology Partner, sales and marketing programmes.

Affiliate may promote other businesses, subject to confidentiality, intellectual-property and other obligations in this Agreement.

7. AFFILIATE LINK

BACI may provide Affiliate with a unique Affiliate Link, referral code or other tracking mechanism.

Affiliate must use the assigned mechanism accurately and must not alter it to falsify attribution.

BACI may replace, rotate or disable tracking mechanisms for technical, security, fraud or programme reasons.

8. ATTRIBUTION

Commission eligibility depends on BACI's reasonable attribution records and applicable Programme rules.

A prospect must ordinarily enter the qualifying journey through Affiliate's valid tracking mechanism or through an approved Qualified Introduction.

BACI may use cookies, referral parameters, programme software, CRM records, sales records or other reasonable evidence to determine attribution.

9. ATTRIBUTION WINDOW

Where BACI uses an attribution window, the applicable duration will be stated in the Affiliate tool, Programme materials or applicable schedule.

BACI will not invent retroactive attribution for a transaction that cannot reasonably be connected to Affiliate.

Where multiple Affiliates claim the same prospect, BACI may apply the applicable documented attribution rule, including first-valid-referral or other reasonable programme logic.

10. EXISTING CUSTOMERS

Unless BACI expressly provides otherwise, Commission is intended for new qualifying customer acquisition.

An existing BACI customer, existing active opportunity or prospect already materially engaged in BACI's sales process may be ineligible for ordinary affiliate Commission.

A new product or expansion opportunity for an existing customer is commissionable only where BACI expressly identifies it as eligible.

11. SELF-REFERRALS

Affiliate may not earn Commission by referring itself, an entity it controls, an entity under common control, or a transaction whose principal purpose is to generate Commission rather than genuine third-party acquisition.

BACI may reject transactions involving Affiliate's own payment method, materially overlapping ownership or other indicators of self-referral.

BACI may approve a disclosed exception in writing where commercially appropriate.

12. EMPLOYEE AND RELATED-PARTY REFERRALS

Affiliate must disclose referrals involving its employees, contractors, household members, controlled entities or other materially related persons where the relationship could affect eligibility.

BACI may exclude such transactions from Commission to prevent artificial acquisition.

Failure to disclose a material related-party relationship may constitute Programme abuse.

13. QUALIFYING TRANSACTION

A transaction qualifies only if the applicable customer is eligible, the transaction is properly attributed, the customer pays BACI for an eligible product, the payment is not fraudulent or reversed, and all other Programme requirements are satisfied.

A signup, lead, trial, demo or quotation alone does not create Commission unless BACI expressly designates that event as commissionable.

14. ELIGIBLE PRODUCTS

Ordinary recurring affiliate Commission applies only to BACI products and access categories BACI designates as eligible.

Under the standard BACI Programme, Enterprise and Technology Partner transactions are excluded from ordinary recurring affiliate Commission.

BACI may add or remove eligible products prospectively through Programme materials or an applicable schedule.

15. STANDARD COMMISSION RATE

For eligible ordinary affiliate transactions, BACI's standard Commission is twenty-five percent (25%) of Eligible Net Subscription Revenue actually received by BACI from the Referred Customer during the first twelve (12) months of that customer's eligible subscription relationship.

The twelve-month Commission period begins on the first eligible paid subscription start date unless the applicable Programme schedule states otherwise.

Commission does not continue indefinitely.

16. ENHANCED COMMISSION RATE

An Affiliate with at least five (5) Active Referred Customers may qualify for an enhanced Commission rate of thirty percent (30%) for eligible Commission arising while the Affiliate satisfies BACI's applicable enhanced-rate criteria.

BACI may verify that the five customers are genuine, independent, active and paid.

The enhanced rate does not apply to transactions otherwise excluded from Commission.

17. ACTIVE-CUSTOMER THRESHOLD

For purposes of the enhanced rate, BACI may measure Active Referred Customers at the time Commission accrues or according to the Programme's reporting cycle.

Cancelled, refunded, fraudulent, delinquent or ineligible accounts may cease to count.

BACI will apply the threshold consistently within the applicable Programme rules.

18. COMMISSION PERIOD

Recurring Commission is limited to the first twelve months of eligible paid subscription revenue for each Referred Customer.

Renewals or payments occurring after the applicable twelve-month period do not generate further ordinary affiliate Commission unless BACI expressly establishes another programme.

A customer's upgrade during the twelve-month period may be commissionable to the extent the upgraded product remains eligible.

19. ELIGIBLE NET SUBSCRIPTION REVENUE

“Eligible Net Subscription Revenue” means eligible subscription Fees actually received and retained by BACI, net of refunds, credits, chargebacks, discounts, taxes, payment reversals and amounts attributable to excluded products or services.

Unless BACI expressly states otherwise, implementation, professional services, taxes, pass-through third-party costs, non-subscription charges and other excluded amounts do not form part of the Commission base.

20. MONTHLY SUBSCRIPTIONS

For an eligible monthly Referred Customer, Commission may accrue on eligible monthly subscription revenue actually received during the applicable twelve-month Commission period.

If the customer cancels, stops paying or becomes ineligible, future Commission stops accordingly.

21. ANNUAL SUBSCRIPTIONS

For an eligible annual Referred Customer, Commission is calculated on eligible subscription revenue actually received and retained by BACI, subject to the twelve-month Commission limit.

Payment timing may follow BACI's receipt and applicable Commission validation or holdback periods.

22. ANNUAL COMMITMENTS PAID MONTHLY

Where an annual BACI commitment is collected in monthly instalments, Affiliate Commission may accrue as eligible instalments are actually received, subject to the Commission period and other requirements.

Affiliate does not acquire a right to unpaid future instalments merely because the customer entered an annual commitment.

23. UPGRADES

An eligible Referred Customer's upgrade during the applicable Commission period may increase the Commission base if the upgraded BACI product remains eligible.

The Commission period does not restart because of an upgrade unless BACI expressly states otherwise.

Enterprise or Technology Partner conversion remains subject to the exclusion below.

24. DOWNGRADES

If a Referred Customer downgrades, future Commission is based on the eligible revenue actually received after the downgrade.

No minimum Commission is guaranteed.

25. ENTERPRISE EXCLUSION

Enterprise subscriptions are excluded from ordinary twenty-five percent or thirty percent recurring affiliate Commission.

An Affiliate that identifies a genuine Enterprise opportunity may instead qualify for BACI's Qualified Introduction arrangement where the requirements below are met.

No Affiliate may claim both recurring affiliate Commission and a Qualified Introduction Fee for the same underlying transaction.

26. TECHNOLOGY PARTNER EXCLUSION

Technology Partner transactions are excluded from ordinary recurring affiliate Commission.

Technology Partner relationships are governed by the Technology Partner Agreement and applicable commercial terms.

An introduction does not create affiliate compensation unless BACI expressly approves a separate written arrangement.

27. QUALIFIED INTRODUCTION PROGRAMME

BACI may pay a fixed Qualified Introduction Fee of two thousand five hundred US dollars (US$2,500) for an eligible introduction that meets BACI's qualification requirements and results in a qualifying completed transaction.

The Qualified Introduction arrangement is principally intended for qualifying opportunities excluded from ordinary recurring affiliate Commission, including eligible Enterprise introductions.

BACI may require pre-registration and approval of the opportunity before substantial BACI sales engagement begins.

28. QUALIFIED INTRODUCTION REQUIREMENTS

A Qualified Introduction must be genuine, specific, attributable to Affiliate and commercially meaningful.

The prospect must not already be an active BACI sales opportunity materially equivalent to the introduction, unless BACI expressly accepts the referral.

Affiliate must provide sufficient information for BACI to identify and assess the opportunity and must have lawful authority to make the introduction.

29. QUALIFIED INTRODUCTION PAYMENT EVENT

The US$2,500 Qualified Introduction Fee becomes payable only after the referred organisation enters the qualifying BACI transaction identified by BACI and BACI receives the required initial payment.

An introduction, meeting, proposal, negotiation or verbal commitment alone does not earn the Fee.

Refunds, fraud, non-payment or reversal may invalidate the Fee as provided below.

30. ACCOUNT-CREDIT OPTION

Where BACI offers the option and Affiliate is itself an eligible BACI customer, Affiliate may elect BACI account credit equal to one-and-one-half times (1.5×) the otherwise payable Qualified Introduction Fee instead of cash.

For a standard US$2,500 Qualified Introduction Fee, the corresponding account-credit option is US$3,750.

The election must be made through the mechanism BACI provides and, once issued, the credit is governed by applicable credit terms.

31. ACCOUNT-CREDIT RESTRICTIONS

Affiliate account credit is non-transferable, has no cash value and may be applied only to eligible BACI charges unless BACI expressly states otherwise.

Credit may not be sold or exchanged.

The Billing Policy governs application of BACI account credits.

32. NO DOUBLE COMPENSATION

The same underlying customer transaction cannot generate both ordinary recurring Affiliate Commission and a Qualified Introduction Fee unless BACI expressly approves an additional incentive in writing.

Where multiple BACI programmes could arguably apply, BACI will determine the applicable programme based on the registered opportunity and commercial relationship.

Affiliate may not restructure a referral to obtain duplicate compensation.

33. DUPLICATE REFERRALS

If more than one Affiliate or partner claims the same prospect or opportunity, BACI may rely on its records and applicable referral-registration rules to determine eligibility.

Ordinarily, the first valid registered or attributable referral satisfying Programme requirements will receive priority unless another programme rule controls.

BACI may reject duplicate or opportunistic claims submitted after an opportunity is already known.

34. SALES-PROCESS CONFLICTS

BACI may reject a referral where the prospect was already in a materially active BACI sales process before Affiliate's attributable involvement.

Affiliate must not interfere with, hijack or re-tag an existing BACI opportunity.

BACI may request evidence of Affiliate's prior relationship or introduction.

35. AFFILIATE ROLE AFTER REFERRAL

Unless BACI requests assistance, Affiliate is not required to participate in BACI's sales process after a referral.

Affiliate has no authority to negotiate BACI terms, pricing, security commitments, implementation obligations or legal provisions.

BACI controls its own sales and contracting decisions.

36. NO GUARANTEE OF CONVERSION

BACI is not obligated to accept, pursue or close any referred prospect.

Affiliate has no Commission right merely because BACI elects not to transact.

BACI retains discretion over customer eligibility, pricing, risk, product fit and contracting.

37. COMMISSION VALIDATION

Commission may be subject to a reasonable validation or holdback period to account for payment settlement, refunds, fraud, chargebacks and attribution review.

The applicable Affiliate tool or Programme materials may identify the payment cycle.

BACI will not knowingly withhold undisputed validated Commission indefinitely.

38. PAYMENT THRESHOLD

BACI may establish a reasonable minimum payout threshold through the Affiliate tool or Programme materials.

Validated Commission below the threshold may roll forward to a later payment cycle.

BACI will disclose an applicable threshold before relying on it to defer payment.

39. PAYMENT METHOD

Affiliate must maintain a supported payment method and accurate payment details.

BACI may use a third-party affiliate or payment platform to administer tracking, tax documentation or payments.

Affiliate is responsible for third-party receiving fees charged to Affiliate unless BACI expressly agrees otherwise.

40. PAYMENT CURRENCY

Commission may be calculated or paid in US dollars or another currency supported by BACI's payment system.

Where currency conversion is required, BACI or its payment provider may use the applicable operational exchange rate.

BACI is not responsible for Affiliate's bank or payment-provider conversion charges.

41. TAXES

Affiliate is responsible for taxes arising from Commission paid to Affiliate, except taxes imposed on BACI's own income.

BACI may require tax forms, taxpayer identification or other documentation before payment.

BACI may withhold amounts where required by law.

42. PAYMENT HOLDS

BACI may temporarily hold payment where reasonably necessary to investigate suspected fraud, attribution manipulation, sanctions, tax-document deficiencies, chargebacks, material breach or payment errors.

BACI should release undisputed amounts when the basis for the hold is resolved.

A payment hold does not itself terminate Programme participation.

43. REFUNDS

If a Referred Customer receives a refund that reduces Eligible Net Subscription Revenue, corresponding Commission may be reduced or reversed.

The same principle applies to a Qualified Introduction Fee if the qualifying transaction is rescinded, fraudulent or materially reversed before the applicable validation point.

BACI may offset a valid reversal against future Commission where lawful.

44. CHARGEBACKS

Transactions subject to chargeback, payment reversal, fraud or non-payment are not eligible to the extent BACI does not retain the underlying revenue.

Commission already paid on such revenue may be reversed or offset.

BACI will correct manifest billing or attribution errors.

45. CREDITS AND DISCOUNTS

Customer credits, discounts and promotional reductions may reduce the Eligible Net Subscription Revenue on which Commission is calculated.

Affiliate may not promise unauthorised BACI discounts.

Only BACI may approve BACI pricing.

46. COMMISSION STATEMENTS

BACI or its Affiliate tool may provide statements showing attributed transactions, status, reversals and Commission.

Affiliate must report a suspected material error within a reasonable period after discovery.

BACI will investigate good-faith discrepancies.

47. BACI RECORDS

BACI's transaction, payment and attribution records will govern Programme calculations absent manifest error.

This does not prevent Affiliate from providing evidence of a genuine tracking failure or administrative mistake.

BACI may correct errors prospectively and, where appropriate, retrospectively.

48. TRACKING TECHNOLOGY

Affiliate must not interfere with BACI tracking technology, overwrite another participant's referral identifier, force cookies, manipulate attribution parameters or use deceptive redirects.

BACI may disregard technically generated attribution where the underlying acquisition is not genuine.

Tracking abuse is a material breach.

49. COOKIE STUFFING

Cookie stuffing, forced clicks, invisible iframes, automatic redirects, pop-under attribution, unauthorised browser extensions or other methods that create attribution without genuine user engagement are prohibited.

Transactions generated through such methods are ineligible for Commission.

50. PAID SEARCH

Affiliate must not present paid advertisements in a manner likely to cause users to believe the advertisement is BACI's own advertisement.

BACI may publish more specific keyword restrictions.

51. DOMAIN NAMES AND SOCIAL HANDLES

Approved descriptive references remain subject to BACI brand guidelines.

52. EMAIL MARKETING

Affiliate must identify itself accurately and provide legally required unsubscribe mechanisms.

53. SMS AND MESSAGING

56. DISCLOSURE OF AFFILIATE RELATIONSHIP

60. TESTIMONIALS AND REVIEWS

61. COUPON AND DEAL SITES

Legitimate educational or service value supplied by Affiliate is not prohibited merely because Affiliate earns Commission.

63. LEAD GENERATION

ANNEX I — STANDARD COMPENSATION SCHEDULE

ORDINARY ELIGIBLE BACI SUBSCRIPTIONS Standard Commission: 25% of Eligible Net Subscription Revenue actually received by BACI. Commission Period: first 12 months of the eligible Referred Customer subscription relationship.

ENHANCED AFFILIATE RATE Rate: 30%. Qualification: at least 5 Active Referred Customers, subject to BACI's validation and applicable Programme rules.

ENTERPRISE Excluded from ordinary recurring affiliate Commission. Eligible qualifying opportunities may instead use the Qualified Introduction programme.

TECHNOLOGY PARTNERS Excluded from ordinary recurring affiliate Commission. Any compensation requires a separate BACI-approved arrangement.

QUALIFIED INTRODUCTION Standard Fee: US$2,500 for an eligible completed qualifying transaction.

OPTIONAL BACI ACCOUNT CREDIT Where offered and elected: 1.5× the otherwise payable Qualified Introduction Fee. Standard US$2,500 Fee therefore corresponds to US$3,750 BACI account credit.

NO DOUBLE COMPENSATION The same underlying transaction does not receive both recurring Affiliate Commission and a Qualified Introduction Fee unless BACI expressly approves otherwise.

ANNEX II — COMMISSION ELIGIBILITY MATRIX

GENERALLY ELIGIBLE — genuine new eligible customer; valid attribution; eligible BACI subscription; successful payment; transaction retained after applicable validation; Affiliate in good standing.

GENERALLY INELIGIBLE — Enterprise recurring Commission; Technology Partner recurring Commission; self-referral; duplicate referral losing under attribution rules; existing materially active sales opportunity; fraudulent transaction; refunded or charged-back revenue; unauthorised coupon or incentive traffic; cookie stuffing; fabricated lead; prohibited marketing source.

CONDITIONAL — existing-customer expansion, upgrades, marketplace purchases, special promotions, reseller involvement, account-credit incentives and other transactions specifically identified by BACI.

The applicable Programme record and commercial schedule control where a transaction has unusual characteristics.

ANNEX III — PROMOTIONAL COMPLIANCE BASELINE

Affiliate should ensure that promotional activity:

1. clearly identifies Affiliate where appropriate; 2. discloses the affiliate relationship where legally required; 3. uses current BACI product information; 4. avoids guaranteed-outcome claims; 5. avoids unauthorised pricing or discounts; 6. complies with email, SMS and privacy law; 7. uses approved BACI marks correctly; 8. avoids trademark bidding where restricted; 9. avoids misleading domains or social handles; 10. does not impersonate BACI; 11. does not use fake reviews or testimonials; 12. does not use cookie stuffing or forced attribution; 13. does not generate fraudulent or incentivised transactions; 14. does not misuse Personal Data; 15. does not interfere with other Affiliates' attribution; and 16. complies with the Acceptable Use Policy.

ANNEX IV — QUALIFIED INTRODUCTION WORKFLOW

A Qualified Introduction should ordinarily follow this sequence:

1. Affiliate identifies a genuine eligible opportunity. 2. Affiliate registers or submits the introduction through BACI's approved channel. 3. BACI checks whether the prospect is already an active opportunity and whether the introduction is eligible. 4. BACI may accept, reject or request additional information. 5. BACI controls its own sales, diligence, pricing and contracting. 6. The prospect executes the qualifying transaction. 7. BACI receives the required initial payment. 8. BACI validates the introduction and absence of disqualifying events. 9. Affiliate receives the US$2,500 Fee or, where available and elected, the 1.5× BACI account-credit alternative. 10. Any later refund, fraud or reversal is handled under this Agreement.

Registration alone does not guarantee payment.

ANNEX V — AFFILIATE / AGENCY / TECHNOLOGY PARTNER DISTINCTION

AFFILIATE — promotes or introduces BACI and earns compensation for qualifying attributable acquisition. Does not act for BACI and ordinarily does not operate client workspaces as part of the Affiliate role.

AGENCY / ADVISER — uses BACI to deliver professional services for clients under the Agency & Adviser Agreement. May charge clients for its own professional services. Affiliate compensation is separate and only applies where the Affiliate Programme requirements are independently satisfied.

TECHNOLOGY PARTNER — integrates BACI with its own technology under the Technology Partner Agreement. Technology Partner transactions are excluded from ordinary recurring Affiliate Commission.

INDEPENDENT PROFESSIONAL — uses BACI for the professional's own permitted business activity under the applicable BACI access category. Multiple client workspaces may require Advisory access.

A participant may have more than one BACI relationship only where each relationship is separately eligible and the participant does not claim duplicate compensation for the same transaction.
