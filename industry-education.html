Education

Education intelligence follows the decisions that release public and institutional budgets, from funding allocations and policy change to procurement, accreditation and framework award. BACI separates the funded obligation from the stated ambition, connecting both to the authorities, institutions, awarding bodies and employers who actually purchase, so businesses can engage while a specification is being written rather than after a supplier has been appointed.

181 Countries · 9 Regions · 12 Sub-sectors

Sub-sectors

01

K-12

School building programmes, curriculum and equipment procurement, framework awards and funding allocations are dated and attributable to a named authority or trust. Enrolment trends and policy consultations move earlier. BACI tracks the funding allocation in particular, because in state education the budget arrives before the buying decision and the suppliers who engage in that window shape what gets specified, and it identifies which authorities, trusts and schools are the actual purchasing entity in a given market.

02

Higher Education

Capital programmes, research grant awards, procurement frameworks and international partnership agreements are dated and name the institution. Enrolment figures and rankings move earlier and describe an institution's position rather than its spending. BACI reads grant awards especially closely, since a funded research programme creates immediate demand for equipment, facilities and specialist services with the money already allocated, and it maps the frameworks a supplier has to sit on before any of it is reachable.

03

EdTech

Institutional adoptions, framework places and trust or district rollouts are dated commitments with a named buyer. Usage figures and classroom pilots are softer, and this is a sector where a teacher trying something and an institution buying it are separated by a long procurement process. BACI keeps those apart, identifying where informal adoption has reached the point of a formal purchase, and which frameworks and approval routes decide whether a product can be bought at all.

04

Vocational & Skills Training

Funding contracts, provider approvals, apprenticeship arrangements and employer commitments are dated and tied to a named funder. Skills shortage data and labour market forecasts move earlier and say where provision will be commissioned next. BACI connects the two, because a documented shortage is what unlocks a funding stream some months later, and identifies the providers, employers and funding routes through which training in a given skill can actually be delivered and paid for.

05

Corporate Learning

The buyer here isn't an educational institution at all, which changes where the signals sit. An employer commissions training because something in the business changed: a regulatory obligation, a systems migration, an acquisition bringing two workforces together, rapid hiring, a compliance failure. BACI reads the corporate world rather than the education one for those moments, since a learning budget is released by a business event and rarely by an educational argument, however good the argument is.

06

Online & Distance Learning

Platform contracts, accreditation of online delivery, institutional partnerships and programme launches are dated and named. Enrolment shifts and completion rates move earlier and indicate whether a model is holding. BACI tracks accreditation especially, because an online programme that isn't recognised can't recruit the students who most need it to be, and identifies the institutions, awarding bodies and partners whose recognition decides whether a course has a market.

07

Early Childhood

Entitlement funding rates, provider registration, setting acquisitions and local commissioning decisions are dated and traceable. Birth rates and participation trends move slowly and say where capacity will be needed years ahead. BACI reads funding rates as the decisive commercial signal, since in this sector the rate a government sets determines whether a setting is viable at all, and it identifies where consolidation, new provision or closure is most likely to follow a change in it.

08

Special Education

Demand here is a legal entitlement rather than a budget decision. A statutory assessment obliges an authority to secure provision whether or not it has the money, which makes the assessment itself the commercial signal and the funding argument a separate, later problem. BACI follows assessments, placement pressure, tribunal volumes and specialist capacity shortfalls, because those show where an authority is legally committed to buying provision it currently can't deliver from its own resources.

09

Language Learning

Institutional contracts, accreditation, government language programmes and corporate agreements are dated and named. Migration flows, trade relationships and inbound investment move earlier and reliably precede demand for a specific language pair in a specific market. BACI reads those patterns alongside the contracts, since a new trade corridor or a wave of relocation creates language demand before anyone commissions provision for it, and identifies who commissions it when they do.

10

Test Prep & Credentialing

Awarding body recognition, syllabus changes, centre approvals and employer or regulator requirements are dated and published. Entry volumes move with them and lag. BACI watches syllabus and requirement changes closely, because a revised qualification obsoletes existing preparation material on a known date and creates a replacement market for everyone supplying into it, and it identifies the awarding bodies, centres and employers whose recognition decides whether a credential carries weight.

11

Study Abroad

This is the only category where the decisive policy is made in a country you aren't operating in. A visa rule, a work rights change or a cap on international enrolment can open or close an entire market within a single announcement, and it does so on the destination government's timetable rather than anyone's commercial one. BACI tracks immigration and enrolment policy across destinations, so agents, institutions and service providers see a corridor closing or opening while there's still time to redirect.

12

Education Publishing

Curriculum adoptions, framework wins, licensing agreements and syllabus changes are dated and name the adopting authority. Budget pressure and the shift to digital move earlier and change what format gets bought rather than whether. BACI works from adoption cycles, since a curriculum decision fixes what a jurisdiction buys for several years and is close to unreachable in between, and identifies when each cycle reopens and which bodies control the decision.
