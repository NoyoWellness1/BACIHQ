CONSULTANT COMPENSATION SCHEDULE

Version 1.0 · Effective Date: 11 September 2026 · Last Updated: 11 September 2026

This Compensation Schedule ("Schedule") states the commission rates, accelerators, qualifying conditions, protection periods and payment rules referred to in the BACI Consultant Terms of Engagement ("Terms"). It is the document the Terms mean when they refer to the "Compensation Schedule".

Capitalised terms not defined here carry the meaning given in the Terms. Where this Schedule and an accepted Engagement Schedule differ, the Engagement Schedule controls for that Consultant.

1. APPLICATION

This Schedule applies to every Consultant engaged under the Terms whose Engagement Schedule identifies a role listed in Part A and states that commission is payable under this Schedule.

This Schedule is prospective. A change issued under section 45 of the Terms does not reduce commission already earned under a prior version, and the version in force when a Qualifying Business first became a paying BACI customer continues to govern that business for its whole commission lifecycle.

2. COMMISSION IS PAID ON MONEY BACI RECEIVES AND KEEPS

Commission is calculated on Eligible Net Revenue actually received and cleared by BACI.

Eligible Net Revenue excludes taxes and similar government charges, refunds, credits, chargebacks, reversed payments, unpaid or written-off invoices, discounts not actually received by BACI, pass-through costs, and any other amount BACI does not retain as revenue.

A signed contract, an issued invoice or a verbal commitment does not earn commission. Money received and retained does.

Where revenue is later refunded, charged back or otherwise not retained, the commission attributable to it is reversed. Where that commission has already been paid to the Consultant, the amount becomes recoverable under section 12.

3. INDIVIDUAL BUSINESS BASIS

Commission is calculated separately for each Qualifying Business a Consultant generates.

Each Qualifying Business has its own commission lifecycle, beginning on the date that business first becomes a paying BACI customer. There is no portfolio-wide commission pool, and a Consultant who generates several Qualifying Businesses may earn separately on each.

4. THE TWO-YEAR ORIGINATING LIFECYCLE

For originating roles, commission runs for two twelve-month periods measured from the date the Qualifying Business first becomes a paying BACI customer.

Year 1 is the first twelve months of that paying relationship. Year 2 is months thirteen to twenty-four, and is payable only while the customer remains active, paid and in good standing.

Originating commission ends on completion of the second twelve-month period. No perpetual commission, residual interest or ownership interest in a BACI customer is created.

A payment received after the second period ends earns no originating commission, and does not begin a further lifecycle for that customer.

If a customer downgrades, commission is calculated on the Eligible Net Revenue actually received at the lower level. If a customer cancels during Year 2, commission ends when qualifying payments to BACI end.

5. TAIL PERIOD AFTER TERMINATION

For the purposes of section 88 of the Terms, this Schedule expressly provides a tail period.

Where a Qualifying Business became a paying BACI customer before the Consultant's engagement ended, the Consultant remains entitled to commission on that business for the remainder of its two-year lifecycle, on the same basis and at the same rates as if the engagement had continued.

That entitlement is commission for business already originated. It is not payment for continuing work, and it does not survive termination for fraud, attribution manipulation or another lawful basis for recovery.

An opportunity that had not become a paying BACI customer before the engagement ended earns no commission, however far advanced it was.

This section does not apply to Account and Renewal Consultants, whose entitlement is addressed in section 9.

6. ACCELERATORS APPLY PROSPECTIVELY

For the purposes of section 44 of the Terms, an accelerator under this Schedule applies PROSPECTIVELY.

A higher rate applies only to Qualifying Business or Eligible Net Revenue arising after the relevant threshold has been reached. It is never applied retrospectively to business or revenue that arose before the threshold was crossed.

Where a single payment takes a Consultant across a threshold, that payment is divided at the boundary: the portion below the threshold is paid at the lower rate and the portion above it at the higher rate. The two portions are recorded separately so the calculation can be reconstructed.

Accelerator rates lift the Year 1 rate, and the renewal and expansion rates, only. They never increase the Year 2 continuity rate.

7. ACCELERATOR PERIODS RUN FROM THE CONSULTANT'S APPOINTMENT

Accelerator performance is measured over successive twelve-month periods beginning on the Consultant's appointment effective date, not the calendar year.

The first period runs from appointment to the day before the first anniversary; each period after that runs a further twelve months from that anniversary. At the start of each period the accelerator measure resets and the base rate applies again until the next threshold is reached.

Resetting does not affect commission already being earned on business generated in an earlier period. A Consultant appointed in October is measured over a full twelve months, not the remainder of the calendar year.

8. ACCELERATOR PERFORMANCE IS NET OF REFUNDS

Accelerator performance is cumulative Eligible Net Revenue, or the cumulative count of Qualifying Businesses, that BACI has actually received and RETAINED during the period.

Where revenue is later refunded or charged back, the Consultant's current accelerator performance falls accordingly and future revenue is paid at whatever band that reduced performance then supports.

A refund does not travel backwards. Commission already earned at a higher rate, on revenue BACI held at the time, is not recalculated, and the record of when a threshold was reached is not altered.

Where a refund reduces performance below a threshold and later revenue takes it above again, the higher rate resumes prospectively.

9. TWO ENTITLEMENTS MAY ARISE ON ONE PAYMENT

An originating Consultant's Year 2 continuity commission and an Account and Renewal Consultant's renewal commission are separate entitlements over the same customer revenue, and both may be payable on the same payment.

The originating Consultant is paid for having brought the customer to BACI. The Account and Renewal Consultant is paid for the work of keeping and growing the account. Neither entitlement reduces the other.

An Account and Renewal Consultant's entitlement arises from a formal account assignment and continues only while that assignment is in force. It is determined by reference to the assignment covering the date the revenue arose, not the assignment in force when the payment happens to be processed.

Where an Account and Renewal Consultant's engagement ends, live account assignments end at the same time and no further renewal or expansion commission accrues on those accounts.

10. RENEWAL AND EXPANSION

Renewal commission is payable on Eligible Net Revenue received on the continuation of an existing account at its existing scope.

Expansion commission is payable only on the incremental Eligible Net Revenue arising where a customer increases its contracted BACI capacity or scope beyond the entitlement it most recently paid for. That includes additional users or seats, additional client workspaces, additional capacity, and a higher access category or operating mode.

Expansion commission applies to the increase only, never to the customer's existing revenue base.

A price increase at unchanged scope is not expansion. Neither is a change in tax, nor a one-off charge that merely makes an invoice larger.

11. ATTRIBUTION

A Consultant must originate, or otherwise qualify for attribution of, the business under the requirements of their role.

Where a role establishes attribution through registered opportunities, the Consultant must register the organisation before approaching it and supply enough information to demonstrate a genuine opportunity. Registration is a claim for review; it is not ownership.

An approved registration carries a protection period of 180 days. Protection may be extended on evidence of current activity and with BACI's approval, but may never extend more than 365 days beyond the date of extension. Protection that has lapsed ceases to protect immediately and the organisation becomes available to other Consultants.

Where a role establishes attribution through tracked referral activity, the first qualifying interaction between a visitor and that Consultant opens a 90-day window. Later interactions within that window are recorded as evidence and do not extend it. Once a window has expired a genuinely new interaction may open a further 90-day window.

Where both a registered opportunity and a referral interaction exist, an approved registration still within its protection period takes precedence, and the competing claim is recorded and reviewed rather than discarded.

No commission arises merely by identifying an organisation already known to, registered with, actively engaged by, or already a customer of BACI.

Attribution is determined as at the date the customer converted, not the date BACI processed the payment.

12. PAYMENT, RECOVERY AND SET-OFF

Commission becomes payable only after BACI has actually received and cleared the corresponding customer payment, and after any applicable validation period.

Payment is made according to the cycle stated in the Engagement Schedule, subject to required tax documentation and lawful withholding.

Where commission has been paid and the underlying revenue is subsequently refunded, charged back or otherwise not retained, the amount becomes recoverable. BACI may recover it by setting it off against future commission, or the Consultant may repay it directly. BACI may also elect to waive recovery, which extinguishes the amount without payment.

A recovery is not treated as recovered until it has been offset, repaid or waived.

13. WHAT DOES NOT EARN COMMISSION

No commission arises on revenue BACI does not retain; on an organisation already a BACI customer; on a self-referral; on an opportunity already under active BACI engagement; on introductions, meetings, proposals, memoranda of understanding or verbal commitments alone; or on any transaction excluded by the Engagement Schedule.

Audience size, impressions, views, followers, engagement, leads and enquiries do not by themselves constitute Qualifying Business. A paying BACI customer must result from the activity.

14. RECORDS

BACI maintains a record of each commission calculation showing the revenue it was calculated on, the rate applied, the plan version in force, the accelerator position at the time, and any subsequent reversal.

A Consultant may request the record relating to their own commission. Corrections are made by posting an adjusting entry rather than by altering an existing one, so the history remains readable.

PART A — ROLES AND RATES

The nine approved roles, their commission bases and their accelerator structures are set out below. All percentages are of Eligible Net Revenue.

Rates in this Part are the rates in force from the Effective Date and are subject to section 45 of the Terms.

1. COMMERCIAL CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: an organisation or independent professional the Consultant brings to BACI that signs up on a Business or Independent Professional plan and starts paying.

Year 1: 12%. Year 2: 5%, while the customer remains active, paid and in good standing.

Accelerator, measured by new Qualifying Businesses in the period:

1 to 5 Qualifying Businesses — 12%

6 to 10 Qualifying Businesses — 15%

11 or more Qualifying Businesses — 17%

2. CHANNEL CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: an advisory firm, agency or consultancy the Consultant brings to BACI that becomes commercially active and generates paying customers.

Year 1: 12%. Year 2: 5%, while the channel remains commercially active.

Accelerator, measured by PRODUCTIVE CHANNELS in the period. A productive channel is a distinct channel partner that has produced qualifying paid BACI business. Twelve customers introduced by one firm is one productive channel, not twelve:

1 to 3 productive channels — 12%

4 to 6 productive channels — 15%

7 or more productive channels — 17%

3. ENTERPRISE CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: an organisation the Consultant brings to BACI that signs an Enterprise agreement and starts paying, where the Consultant materially contributed to developing the relationship.

Year 1: 6%. Year 2: 3%.

Accelerator, measured by new Enterprise Eligible Net Revenue in the period:

Up to US$250,000 — 6%

US$250,001 to US$750,000 — 8%

Above US$750,000 — 9%

4. LICENSING CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: a licensing opportunity the Consultant originates and qualifies that results in an executed BACI licensing agreement and payment to BACI.

Year 1: 8%. Year 2: 4%.

Accelerator, measured by new licensing Eligible Net Revenue in the period:

Up to US$250,000 — 8%

US$250,001 to US$750,000 — 10%

Above US$750,000 — 12%

5. TECHNOLOGY PARTNERSHIP CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: a technology company, platform, integrator or other approved organisation the Consultant brings to BACI that enters a commercially active technology partnership and generates qualifying revenue.

Year 1: 6%. Year 2: 3%.

Accelerator, measured by new partnership Eligible Net Revenue in the period:

Up to US$250,000 — 6%

US$250,001 to US$750,000 — 8%

Above US$750,000 — 9%

6. DEVELOPER ADVOCATE

Qualifying Business: a developer or organisation that starts paying on a Developer plan, demonstrably because of the Consultant's approved technical content, education, community activity, referral, event participation or direct engagement.

Year 1: 10%. Year 2: 5%.

Accelerator, measured by new Developer customers in the period:

1 to 5 Developer customers — 10%

6 to 10 Developer customers — 13%

11 or more Developer customers — 15%

7. ACCOUNT AND RENEWAL CONSULTANT

Entitlement: an existing BACI account formally assigned to the Consultant, where the Consultant materially contributes to retaining, renewing or expanding the relationship. This role has no two-year originating lifecycle; it is paid for active account work while the assignment is in force.

Renewal: 5% of Eligible Net Revenue received on renewal. Expansion: 7% of incremental Eligible Net Revenue, calculated on the increase only.

Accelerator, measured by retained and expanded Eligible Net Revenue in the period:

Up to US$250,000 — renewal 5%, expansion 7%

US$250,001 to US$750,000 — renewal 7%, expansion 9%

Above US$750,000 — renewal 8%, expansion 10%

8. REGIONAL DEVELOPMENT CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: an organisation the Consultant personally brings to BACI within the country, market or professional network they are developing, that becomes a paying customer. The role confers no territorial exclusivity, ownership or licensing rights.

Year 1: 12%. Year 2: 5%.

Accelerator, measured by new Qualifying Businesses in the period:

1 to 5 Qualifying Businesses — 12%

6 to 10 Qualifying Businesses — 15%

11 or more Qualifying Businesses — 17%

9. DIGITAL MARKETING CONSULTANT

Qualifying Business: an organisation that becomes a paying BACI customer directly because of approved digital marketing activity the Consultant undertook, including tracked content, campaigns, links, referral identifiers or other BACI-approved attribution methods.

Year 1: 15%. Year 2: 5%.

Accelerator, measured by attributable new customers in the period:

1 to 10 attributable customers — 15%

11 to 20 attributable customers — 18%

21 or more attributable customers — 20%

PART B — ATTRIBUTION MECHANISM BY ROLE

Commercial, Channel, Enterprise, Licensing, Technology Partnership and Regional Development Consultants establish attribution through REGISTERED OPPORTUNITIES.

Developer Advocates and Digital Marketing Consultants establish attribution through TRACKED REFERRAL ACTIVITY. These roles do not register opportunities.

Account and Renewal Consultants derive entitlement from FORMAL ACCOUNT ASSIGNMENT. They neither register opportunities nor hold referral identifiers.

A role may not establish attribution by a mechanism other than the one stated for it.
